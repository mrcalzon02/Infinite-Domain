# Organic Chemical Secondary Uses

Generated by `scripts/audit_organic_secondary_uses.py` from
`kubejs/config/organic_secondary_uses.json`. Do not hand-edit.

Every organically derived chemical in the metallurgy program carries exactly one secondary use outside metal recovery.

## The three shapes

- **Reduction** - The chemical substitutes for a costly input in a process that already exists; the alternate route is strictly cheaper in the substituted material.
- **Concentration** - The chemical converts a dilute or low-grade stock into a denser one; the effect is an input-to-output ratio greater than one.
- **Multiplier** - The chemical is added to a process that already works without it and multiplies that process's output; the effect is a factor greater than one.

## Invariants

- One chemical, one secondary use, one shape.
- A secondary use never returns metal nuggets, ingots, concentrates, or molten metal.
- A secondary use is available in the era that introduces its chemical.
- No secondary use produces more of its own chemical than it consumes.
- A boosted route never contains every ingredient of its baseline, so a Create basin cannot resolve the weaker recipe by accident.

## Why each chemical earns its second job

- **Fibrous Biomass** (Era 1, reduction): Straw pulps into paper as readily as cane, and the wasteland grows far more wheat than sugar cane. Measured against `minecraft:paper`.
- **Alkaline Botanical Wash** (Era 1, reduction): Plant-ash alkali neutralises wasteland water, so a single lump of charcoal finishes the filtration that previously needed a whole block. Measured against `wastelands:purified_water`.
- **Tannic Pulp** (Era 2, reduction): The pulp is already the graphite binder; doubling the binder charge lets the same blank form around a quarter less refined carbon, and cold. Measured against `infinite_domain:nuclear/bound_graphite_mix`.
- **Tannic Extract** (Era 2, concentration): Tannin is the reagent that turns raw hide into leather, and Era 0 leaves the wasteland saturated with undead flesh and short of cattle. No prior route existed.
- **Fermented Mash** (Era 3, reduction): Spent distiller grain is fertiliser, so the farm stops competing with the bone economy for its own growth. Measured against `minecraft:bone_meal`.
- **Fermented Acid Wash** (Era 3, multiplier): An established acid culture doubles what a basin recovers from the same crop charge, and it does the job of the bone meal the plain fermenter needs. Measured against `createdieselgenerators:basin_fermenting/fermentable`.
- **Pressed Seed Cake** (Era 4, concentration): Seed cake still holds most of its oil; a second compaction concentrates four cakes into the plant oil the biodiesel line runs on. No prior route existed.
- **Saponified Collector** (Era 4, multiplier): A saponified surfactant dissolves the oil the press leaves locked in the seed cell walls, doubling recovered oil from the same oilseed charge. Measured against `infinite_domain:food/crude_vegetable_oil`.
- **Starch Pulp** (Era 5, concentration): Starch plasticised with plant oil is a genuine thermoplastic, and it is the first plastic route that does not begin at an oil well. No prior route existed.
- **Flocculating Solution** (Era 5, multiplier): A starch flocculant drops the brine phase out of crude oil without an electrolyser, so the salt fraction is recovered whole instead of half the time. Measured against `petrochem:electrolyzing/basic_desalting`.
- **Spent Process Solution** (Era 5, concentration): Spent liquor still carries every ion the recovery budget did not claim; carbon adsorption concentrates a litre of it into a quarter litre of feedable slurry. No prior route existed.
- **Kelp Chelating Pulp** (Era 6, multiplier): Kelp is the iodine and alginate source real decontamination medicine is built on; adding it to the canister doubles the doses per batch. Measured against `wastelands:rad_away`.
- **Chelating Broth** (Era 6, concentration): Chelation is the industrial answer to dispersed radionuclides: four blocks of contaminated ground collapse into one controlled uranium fraction and the scrap buried in it. No prior route existed.
- **Orbital Biomass** (Era 7, concentration): Chorus tissue is the only crop that keeps growing off-world; compacted, it is the biomass a habitat biological plant actually feeds on. No prior route existed.
- **Closed-Loop Extract** (Era 7, multiplier): The extract is a regenerable amine wash: it replaces the charcoal sorbent top-up outright and lifts oxygen recovery by half on the same cartridge. Measured against `infinite_domain:space/oxygen/closed_loop_recovery`.
- **Regenerative Catalyst Matrix** (Era 8, reduction): The matrix carries the active sites the extract was only ever a scaffold for, so half the feedstock brews the same litre of reagent without a burner, and it is worth most where the feedstock is scarcest. Measured against `infinite_domain:organic_metallurgy/era_N/reagent`.
- **Regenerative Refining Solution** (Era 8, multiplier): The Era 8 solution is a catalyst carrier: a small dose replaces the sacrificial extract entirely and doubles what the recovery still returns from a spent charge, retroactively closing every partially closed loop below it. Measured against `infinite_domain:organic_metallurgy/era_N/regenerate_reagent`.

## The two Era 8 ladder effects

Both Era 8 chemicals point back at the chemistry ladder itself. Each skips the
era whose own chemical is the catalyst, so neither can return more of that
chemical than it consumes, and each drops an ingredient its baseline requires,
so a basin can never resolve the weaker recipe by accident.

`Regenerative Refining Solution` doses 25 mB into a spent charge, replaces the
sacrificial extract the plain regeneration burns, and doubles the recovered
reagent for Eras 5, 6, 7:

| Era | Reagent | Baseline recovery | Baseline extract | Catalysed recovery | Catalysed extract |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | Flocculating Solution | 50 mB | 1 | 100 mB | 0 |
| 6 | Chelating Broth | 125 mB | 1 | 250 mB | 0 |
| 7 | Closed-Loop Extract | 175 mB | 1 | 350 mB | 0 |

Era 8's own recovery is excluded: doubling it would return more Regenerative
Refining Solution than the dose consumed.

`Regenerative Catalyst Matrix` replaces half the extract charge in reagent brewing for Eras 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 (4 extract -> 2 extract plus one matrix), and the catalysed brew needs no burner. The saving is largest where the feedstock is scarcest:

| Era | Extract | Feedstock | Feedstock saved per 1000 mB reagent |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Fibrous Biomass | `minecraft:wheat` | 4 |
| 2 | Tannic Pulp | `minecraft:oak_log` | 4 |
| 3 | Fermented Mash | `minecraft:sugar` | 4 |
| 4 | Pressed Seed Cake | `minecraft:beetroot_seeds` | 8 |
| 5 | Starch Pulp | `minecraft:potato` | 8 |
| 6 | Kelp Chelating Pulp | `minecraft:kelp` | 8 |
| 7 | Orbital Biomass | `minecraft:chorus_fruit` | 8 |

One matrix costs 8 `minecraft:wheat_seeds`, so the trade is close to even at Era 1 and
strongly favourable by Era 7.
