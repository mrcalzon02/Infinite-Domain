# Technology Reconstruction Chain

Infinite Domain uses selected normally uncraftable technology artifacts as expensive reconstruction milestones. Finding or receiving one through a quest remains the desirable route; sufficiently developed civilization can eventually reproduce it through a deliberately cross-mod recipe.

## Implemented first chain

1. The AE2 Inscriber requires TFMG Steel and a Create Precision Mechanism in addition to its compressed-iron structure and pistons.
2. The Silicon Press can be reconstructed from chromium tooling, Create Precision Mechanisms, charged Certus Quartz, and 1x Compressed Iron Blocks.
3. The Logic Press consumes a Silicon Press and adds Power Grid circuitry, electrum contact blocks, and 2x Compressed Iron Blocks.
4. The Calculation Press consumes a Logic Press and adds charged Certus, sapphire substrate blocks, Power Grid circuitry, and 2x Compressed Iron Blocks.
5. The Engineering Press consumes a Calculation Press and adds an Oritech Machine Core, platinum contact blocks, tungsten tooling, and 2x Compressed Iron Blocks.
6. The normally loot-only Create Cybernetics Netherite QPU requires an Engineering Press, AE2 Engineering Processors, a tier-three Oritech Machine Core, Power Grid circuitry, Netherite Blocks, and 3x Compressed Iron Blocks.
7. The QPU already gates twenty-one high-end cybernetic recipes, so this single reconstruction route makes those implants depend on the preceding Create, TFMG, electrical, AE2, and Oritech chain.

The press sequence is consumptive by design: reconstructing a higher press retools the preceding press. A settlement that needs the earlier press afterward must manufacture another. Quest rewards therefore remain extremely valuable shortcuts without being the only permanent source.

The expanded mineral roles are deliberate: chromium supplies wear-resistant tooling, electrum supplies logic contacts, sapphire supplies a stable calculation substrate, tungsten supplies high-temperature tooling, and platinum supplies advanced contacts. Adamantite and aetherium remain reserved for later nuclear/orbital capstones pending spawn-frequency measurement.

## Reserved late artifacts

- AE2LT Firmament Dust and Floating Matter: orbital and exotic-matter gates.
- Heavy Cores and Echo Shards: high-energy or dimensional machinery.
- Totems of Undying: life-support or resurrection-scale systems.
- Dragon Eggs: singular capstone projects only; never routine machinery.
- Trial Keys: unsuitable as mandatory technology inputs because their distant structure dependence would create an exploration bottleneck rather than an industrial dependency.
