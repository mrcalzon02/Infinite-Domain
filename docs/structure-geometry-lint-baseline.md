# Structure Geometry Lint — Corpus Baseline

Generated by `scripts/structure_geometry_lint.py` (v2 rebuild QA gate) against
all 84 clean-master `.nbt` files in
`kubejs/data/infinite_domain/structure/wasteland/masters/`.

Raw data: `docs/structure-geometry-lint-baseline.json`.

## Headline numbers

- **65 / 84 (77%)** clean masters have at least one hard-fail geometric finding.
- **34,262** individually floating solid blocks (`structural_connectivity`) across those structures.
- **191** unframed/floating glazing findings (`opening_wall_coupling`) across 20 structures.
- **11** furnished-but-sealed (unreachable) rooms (`room_composition`, hard_fail) across 7 structures — see below.
- **77** unfurnished sealed air pockets (`room_composition`, review_flag) across 33 structures — attic-cavity/sealed-vessel candidates, not confirmed bugs.
- **52** unbacked ladder/sign findings (`ladder_backing`) across 5 structures.
- **14 / 9** stair enclosure / stair landing findings.

## Two rounds of self-correction before this baseline was trusted

1. **Per-block glass check → component-based.** The first pass checked every
   individual glass block for an in-plane wall-material neighbor, so any
   contiguous glazing wall (greenhouses, hangar viewing walls) had every
   interior pane flagged, inflating `opening_wall_coupling` to 21,366. Fixed
   by flood-filling glass into connected components and only flagging a
   component that touches zero wall material anywhere on its boundary.
2. **Hardcoded `ground_y=(0, 1)` → per-template dynamic anchor plane.**
   `check_structural_connectivity` originally anchored its flood fill only at
   y=0/1. Quarry/mine/pit/oil-field templates build their real rim and road
   at a `surface` variable well above 0 (8, 10, 12...) and place nothing at
   all at y=0/1, so the fixed anchor plane found **zero anchors** for those
   templates and flagged every solid block in them as floating — a lint
   false positive, not a generator defect. `abandoned_quarry_clean_master`
   dropped from 6,282 (100% of its solid blocks) to 2,363 once the check
   anchors off each template's own lowest solid layer instead. This took the
   corpus-wide floating-block total from 38,360 to the trusted 34,262 above.

Both fixes are in the shipped `scripts/structure_geometry_lint.py`; neither
number above was reported to the user before being caught and corrected.

## Fixed this session (verified end-to-end: patch → re-lint zero findings → regenerate → round-trip reload → re-lint zero findings)

- **`radio_mast_clean_master`**: the antenna base/rod at x=7,z=7 had zero
  physical connection to the four corner-only support legs — nothing
  occupied the center column below y=24. Fixed by bridging the four legs
  through the center with cross-beams at y=24 before the antenna base sits
  down. `scripts/generate_wasteland_sites.py:radio_mast_clean_master()`.
- **`radio_mast` (damage variant)**: its `clear()` call strips the x=8-10

Both fixes are live in `scripts/generate_wasteland_sites.py`, and the
regenerated `.nbt` files are committed to `masters/radio_mast_clean_master.nbt`
and the root-level `radio_mast.nbt` damage variant.

## Room composition — fitness of purpose for size, layout, and access

New check (`check_room_composition`): flood-fills air from the template's
outer boundary, treats whatever air does NOT reach it as an enclosed room,
and tests each enclosed room for (a) at least one door anywhere on its
boundary — a room with none is unreachable by definition, since "enclosed"
already means no other gap exists — and (b) a floor footprint big enough to
be usable (>= 4 floor cells).

**Two false-positive classes were caught and filtered before trusting this
data** (same discipline as the two lint bugs above):

1. **Roof/attic cavities.** `abandoned_culdesac_clean_master`'s gable-roofed
   houses each produced one "sealed room" — but tracing its bounding box
   showed y=10-15, entirely above the shell walls (which top out at y=9).
   It's the attic cavity under the sloped roof, not a room; real buildings
   don't door their attics either.
2. **Intentionally sealed vessels.** `ruined_fuel_depot_clean_master`'s six
   identical 621-block "sealed rooms" turned out to be `round_tank(...)`
   fuel-storage cylinders — correctly modeled as closed vessels, not broken
   rooms. Same pattern in `ruined_grain_elevator_clean_master`'s silos.

Both classes share one trait an authored *purpose-built* room doesn't lack:
nothing inside them is furnished (no chest, bed, machine, radio, appliance).
`check_room_composition` now checks each sealed pocket's boundary for a
furnishing block and only reports `hard_fail` when one is present — strong
evidence a player was meant to enter. An unfurnished sealed pocket is still
reported, but as `review_flag`, since attics and tanks are legitimate but a
human should still glance at the list.

**The 11 hard_fail findings (7 structures) are high-confidence real bugs** —
each is a furnished room with zero access:

| structure | sealed+furnished rooms | note |
|---|---|---|
| destroyed_refugee_convoy_clean_master | 4 | |
| shattered_greenhouse_nursery_clean_master | 2 | two symmetric wings, same bug both sides |
| abandoned_orchard_cannery_clean_master | 1 | |
| battle_tank_clean_master | 1 | hull interior — loot chest, furnace, radio, all unreachable |
| gas_station_clean_master | 1 | |
| ruined_grain_elevator_clean_master | 1 | the one silo with a mechanical press + radio inside — distinct from its 5 empty (legitimately sealed) neighbors |

The 33-structure / 77-pocket review_flag list is in
`docs/structure-geometry-lint-baseline.json` under
`unfurnished_sealed_review_flag_by_structure` — worth a pass to confirm each
really is an attic/vessel and not a room the furnishing heuristic missed,
but none of it is being treated as confirmed defect count.

A performance bug was also caught and fixed while building this check:
the floor-footprint computation recomputed `min(ys)` inside a per-cell set
comprehension instead of once per room, making the check accidentally
O(n²) in room size. `collapsed_subway_station_clean_master`'s one large
interconnected component (36,772 air blocks) took 17 seconds before the
fix and 0.15 seconds after — the full 84-file corpus run went from timing
out past 2 minutes to completing in under a minute.

## Next concrete lead (diagnosed, not yet fixed)

`excavator_pit_clean_master`'s 6,842 floating blocks survive the ground-plane
fix, so they're real. Root cause traced to
`scripts/extraction_family.py:excavator_pit_clean_master()`: the boom/bucket-
wheel assembly starts at x=36 with nothing bridging it to the machinery
deck's edge at x=31 — a 5-block gap with no connecting geometry, so the
entire boom-and-wheel structure is unsupported. This is a genuine authored-
support gap, not a one-line fix like `radio_mast`, and is the next highest-
leverage item once family-by-family v2 remediation starts on the extraction
family.
