# Continuity Off-World Expansion — Mercury and Venus

Generated by `scripts/generate_continuity_offworld_expansion.py`.

Mercury and Venus previously had zero discoverable structures despite both being real,
walkable Stellaris landing sites. This pass gives each one a single Continuity site,
using the same lightweight Template/NBT pipeline as the "alien" structure family
(direct jigsaw/structure_set/template_pool registration, no masters/variants split) —
not the full StructureSmith/lint-verified pipeline used for the hero Continuity
Far-Side Redoubt and Lunar Crash Station on the Moon.

## Continuity Solar Forge Outpost (Mercury)

- `infinite_domain:offworld/continuity_mercury_outpost`, 29×16×29, restricted to
  `#stellaris:mercury_biomes`.
- A pressurized solar/geothermal relay outpost caught mid-collapse rather than long
  dead: interior consoles and a coal generator are still lit, cargo tanks still
  standing, but a fresh lava breach is eating diagonally through one roof corner and
  down the outer wall, cracking the mercury-stone bricks and killing half the exterior
  solar array. The antenna mast is snapped, one section lying in the regolith nearby.
- Reuses existing items for loot rather than inventing a new relic: guaranteed 1-3
  rolls from `infinite_domain:chests/offworld/continuity_mercury_outpost_salvage.json`
  (radiation laminate, carbon scrubber, mercury uranium ore, iron ingots).
- Placement: `random_spread`, spacing 56 / separation 22 / salt 1732051001.
- Locate: `/locate structure infinite_domain:offworld/continuity_mercury_outpost`

## Continuity Descent Wreck (Venus)

- `infinite_domain:offworld/continuity_venus_descent_wreck`, 17×11×17, restricted to
  `#stellaris:venus_biomes`.
- Small and recent, deliberately not ancient: an uncrewed Continuity survey probe
  crushed nose-first into a scorched crater, hull torn open in several places, one
  intact top hatch and ladder down into the crew-less interior. This is the pack's
  "recent crashed remnants of Explorers" counterpart to the ancient Venusian Pressure
  Shrine already on the same planet — plain wreckage, no myth, no relic.
- Loot: guaranteed 1-3 rolls from
  `infinite_domain:chests/offworld/continuity_venus_descent_wreck_salvage.json`
  (sensor package, redstone, heavy metal ingots).
- Placement: `random_spread`, spacing 42 / separation 16 / salt 1732051002 — deliberately
  closer together than the Pressure Shrine (48/18) since it's meant to read as an
  everyday accident, not a landmark.
- Locate: `/locate structure infinite_domain:offworld/continuity_venus_descent_wreck`

## Required runtime gate

Neither site has been tested in a live world. Before treating either as finished,
verify on fresh Mercury/Venus worlds: jigsaw placement, surface projection and terrain
blending (particularly the Mercury lava breach — confirm it doesn't flood the interior
or block the chest), no invalid Stellaris blockstates, and traversable interiors.
