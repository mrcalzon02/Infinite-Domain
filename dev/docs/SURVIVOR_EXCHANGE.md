# Survivor Exchange

The Survivor Exchange is a separate FTB Quests shop group. Purchases consume Create Numismatics currency and can be repeated after a one-tick cooldown. Each purchase supplies one spawn egg, so establishing a breeding pair normally costs twice the listed amount.

## Early livestock prices

| Animal | Price per egg | Spur value | Progression value |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sheep | 1 Bevel | 8 | Wool, beds, mutton |
| Pig | 2 Bevels | 16 | Renewable food |
| Chicken | 2 Sprockets | 32 | Eggs, feathers, compact food production |
| Rabbit | 3 Sprockets | 48 | Hide, feet, specialized food |
| Cow | 1 Cog | 64 | Leather, milk, beef |
| Goat | 2 Cogs | 128 | Milk and renewable horns |

Numismatics values used by the pack are Spur 1, Bevel 8, Sprocket 16, Cog 64, Crown 512, and Sun 4096.

Horses are intentionally excluded from the early shop. Mounts belong with later transport and logistics progression rather than basic food-and-material recovery.

The live chapter is `config/ftbquests/quests/chapters/early_livestock_exchange.snbt`.
