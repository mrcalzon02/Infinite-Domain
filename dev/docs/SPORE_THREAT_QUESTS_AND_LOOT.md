# Spore Threat Dossier and Structure Loot

Date: 2026-08-14

Infinite Domain uses Fungal Infection: Spore 2.2.0j on Minecraft 1.21.1. The
infection advances in the world independently of the civilization quest eras, so
its quest chapter has no era-stage dependency and remains visible at all times.

## Persistent threat chapter

`The Rot — Spore Threat Dossier` contains 38 quests, including 27 real FTB Quests
kill tasks. Its sequence covers field preparation, infected hosts, evolved and
hyper-evolved organisms, organoids, calamities, and a final Proto Hivemind kill.
Material collection and consumed analysis samples connect the combat steps.

Six non-craftable sample items are added through KubeJS. Matching Spore entities
drop them without replacing their native drops. Common specimens are probabilistic;
Calamities and the Proto Hivemind provide guaranteed samples. Analysis turn-ins
award progressively stronger AE2 hardware and cyberware, with the encounter's
difficulty acting as the practical gate rather than civilization stages.

The chapter and localization are generated by
`scripts/generators/build_spore_threat_quests.py`. Entity identifiers and localized names
were verified against the installed Spore jar, not inferred from an older mod
version.

## Structure loot policy

All five Spore structure chest tables are overridden at their original resource
locations. The structures therefore continue requesting the same named tables,
but their contents are limited to Era 1–3 survival and containment supplies.

- Document chests retain paper, books, Documents, and rare Circuit Boards, with
  a very rare Enviromine PDA.
- Equipment chests contain restrained iron equipment, arrows, bandages, Gas
  Masks, Air Filters, Enviromine ventilation pipes/intakes/units, Syringes,
  Compound Plates, rare Circuit Boards, and a very rare Scanner.
- Food chests prioritize canned food, Purified Water, ordinary cooked food,
  bread, milk, and bandages. Golden Apples were removed.
- Ice chests retain ice and Ice Canisters at sharply reduced quantities. The
  high-tier P.C.I. was removed.
- Organ chests retain their anatomical identity, but previous stacks as large as
  fifteen were reduced to small specimen quantities.

The Spore Reaver and Syringe Gun were removed from structure loot because they
skip intended combat progression. No midgame AE2, Oritech, nuclear, orbital, or
endgame equipment is introduced by these tables.

`scripts/generators/build_spore_structure_loot.py` validates every selected item against the
installed registry and writes an exact weighted-entry index to
`docs/spore-structure-loot/effective-spore-chest-loot.csv`.
