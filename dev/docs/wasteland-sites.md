# Overworld Wasteland Sites

Generated by `scripts/generate_wasteland_sites.py` into the KubeJS data pack. These are ordinary Minecraft 1.21.1 compressed structure templates placed through jigsaw pools and random-spread structure sets.

Every surface site uses `beard_box` terrain adaptation to feather terrain into the complete footprint and provide natural-looking support around roads, walls, and large foundations. Underground caches and bunker networks use `bury` adaptation so surrounding terrain closes around their reinforced shells.

No Spore blocks, entities, pools, structures, or biome tags are used.

The custom abandoned-site templates themselves remain Spore-free. Separately, native Spore landmarks retain their three-per-ring placement and now also receive low-frequency random-spread placement outside the rings: a weighted surface pool at 42/16 spacing, cold mines at 84/30, and frozen-ocean iceberg mines at 96/34. Their surface eligibility includes vanilla plains, forests, taigas, savannas, jungles, deserts and badlands as well as all Wastelands land biomes.

### Thirty-structure Ruined City expansion

The following are additive `wastelands:city` structures divided into six independent placement families. They approximately double pack-wide city variety while also increasing actual encounter density.

- Civic: ruined hospital, fire station, police precinct, courthouse, and emergency relief shelter.
- Transit: buried collapsed subway station, ruined bus terminal, freight depot, elevated railway collapse, and sunken highway interchange.
- Commercial: shopping mall, department store, bombed hotel, buried bank vault, and ruined office tower.
- Residential/social: tenement courtyard, rowhouse block, shattered luxury condominium, city school, and community center.
- Utilities: electrical substation, water-treatment plant, district-heating station, municipal incinerator, and fuel depot.
- Pack-themed: cyberware clinic, AE2 records archive, Create factory, collapsed airship terminal, and nuclear research annex.

Common city families use 22–27 chunk spacing. Pack-themed landmarks use 42-chunk spacing. The subway is buried; every other site uses `beard_box` terrain feathering.

## Biomes

Sites generate in the selected land-biome tag `#infinite_domain:wasteland_site_biomes`: plains, forests, taigas, savannas, deserts, badlands, meadow, sparse jungle, the three land biomes from The Wasteland Reworked, plus `wastelands:apocalypse`, `wastelands:forest`, and the ruined-city biome `wastelands:city`. Oceans, rivers, beaches, mountains, caves, the Nether, the End, space dimensions, and Spore-specific placement are excluded.

Worldgen changes apply only to newly generated chunks.

## Danger and loot

The original hostile sites use deliberately slow zombie and mutant spawners. The buried survivor cache and bunker network are intentionally much more dangerous and contain multiple `minecraft:pillager` spawners.

- `minecraft:zombie`
- `the_wasteland_reworked:ghoul`
- `the_wasteland_reworked:irradiated`
- `the_wasteland_reworked:mutant`
- `mutantmonsters:mutant_zombie`

Loot is split into roadside, household, market, industrial, office, military, survivor-cache, and farm tables. It emphasizes food, water, cloth, bandages, scrap, pipes, ammunition, filters, seeds, and modest industrial components rather than progression-skipping machinery.
