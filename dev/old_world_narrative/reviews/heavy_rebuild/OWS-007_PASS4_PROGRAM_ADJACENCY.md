- a north public/administrative threshold;

## Central controlled-trial wing

The main donor hall should be reorganized around repeatable experimental bays rather than broad generic rooms.

Required trial modules:

- **reference cultivation:** untreated control lots under commercial target conditions;
- **drought / nutrient-interruption cell:** dry/root-zone stress with recovery staging;
- **heat / cold cycling cell:** controlled temperature stress;
- **salinity / substrate-tolerance cell:** altered growing medium and nutrient conditions;
- **spore-survival suite:** more tightly bounded drying/air/surface-persistence work;
- **sample-transfer and observation spine:** controlled pass-throughs, consoles and visual comparison;
- **shared chamber service back:** environmental supply/return, nutrient/water feeds and maintenance clearance.

The cells should share structural rhythm and service logic so a player understands they are a comparative program. Their internal conditions and transfer points must differ enough to prevent cloned-room reading.
