## Identity premise

OWS-002 must read as a municipal emergency conversion operated with Verdant Continuum Foods systems. Neither layer is sufficient by itself.

The civic shell communicates familiarity and public legitimacy. The VCF layer communicates the cultivation technology and supply system. The emergency layer communicates controlled relief throughput.

## Required primary identity

The intact D0 building requires supported readable installations containing:

- `VERDANT CONTINUUM FOODS`
- `EMERGENCY COMMUNITY GROW HALL`

These should occupy the north public facade/lobby hierarchy, not random interior walls.

VCF may also appear as a smaller abbreviation on service equipment, but abbreviation alone does not satisfy the primary identity requirement.

## Sign support correctness

Every wall sign must be mounted one block off a real supporting face using the established wall-sign helper pattern. Gate B should assert:

- support block exists;
- sign position is air before placement;
- sign does not intersect canopy, crop rack, pipe or structural frame;
- facing is consistent with the viewer/route it serves.

Unsupported or wall-plane signs are a hard review failure.

## Material / color identity

VCF:

- white and light-gray cleanable surfaces;
- lime as restrained identity/process accent;
- glass at public/control interfaces;
- biological-service equipment rather than generic lab props.

Emergency/municipal:

- localized yellow routing/hold markings;
- neutral durable civic surfaces;
- queue/authorization language;
- practical relief-dispatch markings.

Retained civic building:

- masonry/brick body;
- community-center window rhythm;
- recognizable lobby and hall proportions.

## Anti-patterns

Reject the D0 identity if it becomes:

- `community center + green stripe`;
- generic white laboratory;
- generic warehouse with crops;
- emergency bunker / military checkpoint;
- fungus imagery everywhere;
- corporate branding replacing operational signage;
- labels floating on unsupported faces;
- every room marked by the same lime color regardless of purpose.

## Historical layering constraint

D0 signage represents normal early-containment operation. Later D1 overlays may add temporary hold/routing notices or exception markers, but they must not retroactively erase the normal system.

D3 decay may damage some identity while preserving enough surviving VCF/emergency signage for the original institutional story to remain readable.

## Proof relationship
