- received viable culture stock from upstream producers;
- assigned batch identity and licensing destination;

- inspected and quarantined suspect seals or batches;
- maintained refrigeration and environmental-control plant;
- packed and dispatched viable cultures into a wider distribution network;
- encountered an early material/gasket anomaly while the broader system was still functioning.
