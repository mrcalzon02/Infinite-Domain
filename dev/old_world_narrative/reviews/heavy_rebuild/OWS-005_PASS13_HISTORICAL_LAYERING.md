The facility is abandoned with the unresolved D1 intervention still visible. Weather then exploits the systems already under stress:

- the line-02 roof sanitation tank/header fails and creates a wet-service damage path;
- the refrigeration-B bank fails above cold hold B, opening localized roof and ceiling damage;
- the rear packing monitor loses coherent panels and drops debris onto packing line 02;
- drainage failure produces moss and sediment along the wet/reject route;
- receiving canopy bay 02 loses limited roof/support material while bay 01 remains reconstructable;
- public/QA frontage, proof route, line 01 and the overall raw-to-cold sequence survive.

D2 is omitted because the site's failure is a slow quality incident, withdrawal and weathering sequence rather than a separate explosive collapse event. D1 and D3 are the useful historical comparison states.
