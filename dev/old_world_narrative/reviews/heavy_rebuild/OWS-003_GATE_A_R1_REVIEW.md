## Non-blocking carry-forward requirements for Passes 7–12 / Gate B

Gate A approves only the massing. The following remain mandatory intact-state work:

1. **Roof plant must become a real system.** Equipment masses need service trunks, controls and access tied to the cold vault/nursery below.
2. **Roof-light strips must align to structure/interior program.** They cannot remain merely top-surface graphics.
3. **Receiving threshold must open into an actual conditioned receiving/check sequence.**
4. **Dispatch threshold must open into outbound cold staging and conditioned packing.**
5. **Large brick bays need purpose-driven openings/service articulation.** Do not decorate broad walls randomly; openings must correspond to cold rooms, service corridors or technical spaces.
6. **Maintenance tower must contain a usable vertical route and landing.** Gate A protects the mass only.
7. **The orchard must remain subordinate.** Later detail must not re-grow it into the visual hero.
8. **The front/admin annex needs real batch/licensing/release functions.** Its current clean facade is only massing evidence.
9. **Cold vault and dormancy nursery must become visibly different but connected operational spaces.**
10. **No sign-only VCF identity.** Institutional character must emerge from controlled cold-chain architecture, batch workflow and technical service logic.
