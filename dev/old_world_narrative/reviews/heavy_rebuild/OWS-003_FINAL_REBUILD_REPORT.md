## Rebuild intent

The shipping donor began as an abandoned orchard cannery with only a thin VCF overlay: facade color bands, a small glazed nursery insert, a few cooler/crate rows and a proof chest. Phase 0 correctly rejected that state because the cannery remained the building while the supposed cold-chain nursery existed only as decoration inside it.

The heavy rebuild retained the useful agricultural-industrial bones—brick processing hall, lower administrative annex, orchard relationship and repeated roof-light rhythm—while rebuilding the program around a regional cold-chain biological-stock workflow.

## Real-world functional precedent

Pass 3 used cold-chain logistics, genebank/culture-storage and controlled biological-distribution practice as functional precedent. The resulting architecture treats documentation and batch identity as inseparable from stored material, separates suspect temperature/seal events from released stock, provides monitored conditioned storage, and makes outbound distribution a controlled release process rather than generic warehousing.

## Final architecture and workflow

The completed facility supports the sequence:

`conditioned receiving -> intake/pre-cool -> cold vault -> dormancy nursery -> batch inspection -> quality hold/release -> licensed records -> conditioned packing -> outbound dispatch`.

The converted cannery now includes:

- a recognizable public/admin and batch-records front bar;
- a dominant high cold-chain hall rather than a generic processing shed;
- dense conditioned cold-vault storage;
- three controlled dormancy nursery cells;
- protected inspection/service floors and a central operations spine;
- separated quality-hold and released-stock logic;
- conditioned receiving/freight access distinct from outbound dispatch;
- batch/release/licensing records where the canonical evidence is stored;
- a physically connected rooftop refrigeration plant and maintenance route;
- retained orchard rows as older site history rather than active production or visual concealment;
- VCF retrofit identity layered over surviving cannery masonry rather than replacing the donor with a generic corporate box.

## Environmental narrative

Without reading the proof item, the player can infer that this was not a farm or supermarket. Cultures arrived under controlled logistics, entered monitored cold storage, moved through nursery/inspection/hold functions, were associated with batch records and license routing, and left through a separate conditioned dispatch chain. The localized D1 quality exception implies staff initially treated early degradation as a manageable materials problem rather than evidence of civilization-scale failure.

The later D3 ruin follows loss of refrigeration maintenance, roof/service penetration failure, dock/weather exposure and long abandonment. The building remains reconstructable enough for the player to understand what it once did.
