# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-001 — Pass 15 Encounter Architecture

## Encounter role

OWS-001 is a **common narrative discovery site**, not a combat arena. Its encounter architecture must support tension and incidental hostile occupation without sacrificing the player's ability to reconstruct the building's original workflow.

## Navigation priorities

- Preserve the main public entrance as the obvious discovery route.
- Preserve the central staff spine as the most legible interior navigation axis.
- Preserve at least one alternate exit through the rear receiving/service edge.
- Keep the cold-side and returns-side routes distinct enough that players can choose which operational story to investigate first.
- Do not place guaranteed hazards directly on the deterministic proof location.

## Suitable threat spaces

### West quality-hold / return annex

This is the best optional close-range tension pocket because it is separated from the public room, has real service boundaries and can contain debris without destroying the main route. Later D3 dressing may create broken sightlines or an incidental hostile nest here.

### Rear receiving

The loading aperture and freight apron can support long sightlines and exterior-to-interior pressure. Debris should create cover without blocking the three-block staff spine or the path to records.

### East cold holding

The repeated cold-storage geometry can create constrained sightlines, but the three-block locker/service aisle is a protected operational requirement. No encounter dressing may reduce it below the approved clearance.

## Explicit encounter decisions

- **No mandatory spawner is required** for OWS-001.
- Ambient world hostiles are sufficient for a common early-story site unless a later pack-wide encounter system requires a specific occupant profile.
- If a deterministic encounter is added later, it must be low-intensity and tied to a believable D3 occupation pocket rather than distributed evenly through every room.
- Do not convert counters, staff doors or proof access into one-block combat traps.

## Failure condition

If combat dressing makes the original circulation or functional zoning harder to understand than it was at Gate B, the encounter pass fails.
