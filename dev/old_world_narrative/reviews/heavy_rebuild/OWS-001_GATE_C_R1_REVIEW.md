**PASSED.** OWS-001 may advance beyond the D0/D1/D3 historical-state gate.

Gate C r1 derives all three states from the accepted Gate-B r3 architecture and preserves the same fixed camera set. The review implementation satisfies the recorded Pass 13–18 historical, environmental, encounter, loot, quest-proof and causal-decay contracts without using generic ruin noise to replace the building's original program.

## State separation

### D0 — normal operation

D0 is the accepted Gate-B r3 Verdant Continuum Foods Neighborhood Culture Service Depot. No historical damage is back-projected into normal operation.

### D1 — early anomaly

D1 changes **29 structure positions**. The changes stay inside the normal quality-control and maintenance workflow: west quality-hold pressure, additional sanitation replacement stock, one cold-service inspection interface and accumulating receiving/batch exceptions.

Image-level QA confirms that this state is intentionally not an exterior disaster signal. The four exterior isometric cameras are unchanged, while the label-free horizontal floor maps change by **0.0165615142 (1.66%)** and the interior cutaway changes by **0.0025592653 (0.26%)**. This is appropriate for a common Pre-crisis → Early anomaly site whose workers still understand the problem as maintenance and quality control rather than societal collapse.

### D3 — centuries-later ruin

D3 changes **137 structure positions** from D0. The physical failures are causal and concentrated:

- one roof/cold-service failure interrupts the refrigeration/service zone and creates water-related damage below;
- one cold-bank segment fails while enough repeated equipment survives to explain the original cold chain;
- the west wet-service/return annex develops localized roof, wall and floor decay;
- the rear freight edge accumulates weather exposure and floor deterioration without blocking the staff spine;
- limited public glazing is lost while the entrance and primary VCF identity survive;
- the deterministic proof chest remains in the supervisor/batch-record room with the correct Old World loot table.

Rendered-pixel QA measures D3 at **0.0164376620 (1.64%) average visible change across the five isometric review views** and **0.0487804878 (4.88%) change in label-free horizontal floor maps**. Every exterior camera registers D3 change, ranging from roughly **1.41% to 1.89%**. Exterior foreground/silhouette retention remains effectively 100%, which confirms that decay reads as damage to the approved building rather than replacement of it with a rubble mass.

## Narrative judgment

The progression is correct for OWS-001. D1 communicates that ordinary return, seal, sanitation and cold-chain maintenance is becoming strange without making the depot look like a quarantine fortress. D3 communicates centuries of abandonment, weather and failed maintenance while leaving the original consumer-scale culture-service operation reconstructable.

There is no blanket mycelium treatment, no crater, no military destruction, no mandatory combat conversion and no emergency-authority redesign. The site continues to prove that Evercrop culture distribution was ordinary and widespread before later Old World locations explain the deeper causal mechanism.
