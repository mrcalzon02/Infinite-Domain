## Review basis

An independent `ows-visual-reviewer` verified the untouched shipping provenance and inspected the contact sheet plus every persisted fixed-camera, roof and cutaway artifact. The evidence is sufficient to close Phase 0; this decision does not approve any later gate.

## Useful donor elements

- compact 41 x 15 x 33 scale suits a common roadside site;
- three broad vehicle openings immediately communicate a garage;
- a separate corner pedestrian entrance can seed customer/staff access;
- clear-span hall and repeated lane spacing can support a short repair workflow;
- rear stock/cage adjacency can support controlled parts issue, records and deterministic proof;
- flat roof and blade/datum can support stronger Atlas identity and connected services;
- the intact pre-crisis presentation is appropriate.

## Visible rebuild defects

- massing is a nearly featureless masonry box with blank side/rear elevations, negligible depth, structural rhythm, service thresholds or roadside hierarchy;
- roof slabs, isolated central block and rear blade read as detached decoration rather than connected exhaust, power, air, lifting or maintenance systems;
- Atlas identity depends on applied bands and props instead of standardized framed bays, maintainable machine cells and industrial service anatomy;
- only two of three lanes contain small press/depot pairs, and no credible intake/diagnosis-to-repair-to-calibration-to-release workflow exists;
- lifts, pits, overhead handling, guarded machinery, tools, diagnostics, calibration and outbound inspection are absent or visually unconvincing;
- customer, vehicle, technician, parts, waste/rework and release circulation are conflated;
- rear parts/proof furnishing does not read as a controlled issue-and-custody room;
- site treatment lacks recovery apron, customer drop-off, parts delivery, outbound queue, drainage, utilities and refuse/rework handling;
- floor slices expose a largely empty upper shell;
- the result reads as a generic garage with Create props rather than an Atlas automated roadside repair depot.

## Phase-0 disposition

Retain the compact scale, three-bay concept, pedestrian threshold, hall proportions, rear proof adjacency and normal-operation phase as donor information. Rebuild site organization, architecture, circulation, repair workflow, automation, roof/service systems and institutional identity from the program outward.

**BASELINE SUFFICIENT. REBUILD REQUIRED.**
