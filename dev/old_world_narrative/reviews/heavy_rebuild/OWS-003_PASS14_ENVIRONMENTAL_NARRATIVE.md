# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-003 Pass 14 — Environmental Narrative

**Target:** OWS-003 — Verdant Continuum Foods Cold-Chain Culture Nursery  
**Status:** COMPLETE — planning only until Gate-C implementation

## Narrative purpose

OWS-003 connects Evercrop cultivation to dormancy, licensing and worldwide logistics. Before the player reads any proof item, the building itself should communicate that biological cultures were treated as standardized stock moving through a regional cold chain.

## What the environment should communicate without text

### Ordinary scale and professionalism

- repeated cooler banks and standardized nursery cells imply routine volume rather than experimental scarcity;
- receiving, check, hold, storage, release, packing and dispatch are physically different stages;
- the retained orchard/cannery fabric shows VCF occupying and converting an older food-processing site rather than building an isolated secret laboratory;
- rooftop refrigeration and distributed service piping show the energy/mechanical burden of keeping the biological logistics network alive.

### Global routing

- batch registration and licensing control sit upstream of release;
- conditioned packing and outbound staging are separate from storage;
- pallet/crate placement belongs at real handoff points;
- VCF route/batch color language should make stock movement legible even when signs are damaged.

### Early anomaly

D1 environmental evidence should show a normal organization trying to handle an abnormal but still apparently bounded material problem:

- one seal/gasket interface receives repeated attention;
- selected stock is routed to quality hold rather than destroyed wholesale;
- one nursery/service segment receives yellow exception coding;
- spare maintenance material appears near the affected service node;
- the rest of the facility remains productive.

### Final abandonment

D3 should let the player reconstruct the order of failure:

1. refrigeration and roof-service systems stop being maintained;
2. weather enters through roof lights/service penetrations;
3. conditioned zones progressively lose integrity;
4. dock edges and exposed logistics spaces deteriorate;
5. the old orchard becomes irregular and untended;
6. records and surviving cold-chain architecture outlast the company that operated them.

## Avoided storytelling shortcuts

Do not use:

- fungus/mushroom infestation as generic visual shorthand for Evercrop;
- blood, quarantine cages or extreme biohazard language in D1;
- unexplained explosions;
- random missing walls unrelated to water, service failure or exposed openings;
- mass corpse scenes;
- active Darknet equipment or messages.

## Pass 14 decision

**ENVIRONMENTAL NARRATIVE: COMPLETE FOR GATE-C IMPLEMENTATION.**

The physical story is standardized biological logistics first, localized material anomaly second, and long-term cold-chain/weather failure last.
