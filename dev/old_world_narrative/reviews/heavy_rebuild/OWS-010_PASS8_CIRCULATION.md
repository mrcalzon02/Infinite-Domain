# OWS-010 Pass 8 — Circulation and Access

**Target:** OWS-010 — Atlas Conveyor Transfer Hall  
**Status:** implemented in Gate-B r1 candidate

Five route families are explicit and separated:

1. staff enter from the north threshold into security/dispatch and the west annex;
2. operators use a protected three-block ground gallery parallel to all four lanes;
3. a full two-wide annex stair reaches control, an upper landing and the guarded two-wide cross-aisle catwalk;
4. maintenance staff enter independently from the east, use a second two-wide stair to the catwalk, and continue on a paired stair to roof service;
5. a depressed guarded drive trench parallels every lane beneath the raised outbound return and connects back to the east core.

The four freight portals remain unobstructed. North staff, east service/emergency and dock thresholds are independent. No principal route uses a ladder, jump, conveyor bed or one-block choke.
