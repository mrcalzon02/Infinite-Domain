## Institutional purpose

OWS-004 is the principal physical proof that Evercrop/mycological agriculture was not a small laboratory curiosity. It was industrialized vertically, integrated into a high-throughput urban building and presented publicly as an optimistic answer to food production constraints.

The finished site must read as a purpose-converted or purpose-adapted vertical agricultural tower with a complete production system, not as an office tower containing mushroom rows.

Required narrative/functional signals already established by the target registry are:

- visible VCF cultivation identity and a luminous rooftop greenhouse/cultivation cap;
- a public demonstration / visitor threshold that communicates confidence and normal commercial adoption;
- four legible cultivation floors;
- nutrient/service risers and floor-by-floor utility distribution;
- mycelial grow rows, nutrient tanks, harvest transfer and packaging systems;
- lower productive floors contrasted with an upper floor that later enters active containment;
- deterministic `kubejs:evercrop_cultivation_handbook` evidence and associated 01a/01b audio-diary material;
- retained separation between static schematic approval and runtime/worldgen approval.

## Donor audit

The office-tower donor contributes several useful pieces that should not be discarded merely because the current execution is poor:

- real high-rise verticality and a multi-story floor stack suitable for repeated production modules;
- a podium/tower relationship that can support public demonstration, receiving, support and logistics below controlled production floors;
- enough vertical-access span to support an actual service/core hierarchy;
- a roof large enough to carry greenhouse, ventilation, nutrient and maintenance systems;
- structural repetition that can be converted into cultivation-bay rhythm rather than office-window rhythm.

Those are useful bones, not an architectural answer.

## Shipping implementation audit

The existing functional implementation already has substantial mechanical coverage: four cultivation floors, mycelial crop rows, nutrient tanks, harvest depots, service/riser elements, packaging stock, an upper-floor yellow containment field, rooftop cultivation, deterministic proof and long vertical access. The generated record reports 48,604 placed blocks, 204 functional fixtures, 22 working doors and a 34-block vertical-access span.

That functional density is worth preserving conceptually. It does not justify preserving the current architectural expression.

The legacy implementation remains dominated by the donor because most interventions are thin overlays:

- green horizontal bands and front color fields applied to an office-tower shell;
- crop strips repeated inside otherwise generic floors;
- small tanks/depots placed as equipment islands rather than as a building-wide agricultural utility system;
- a rooftop crop patch/box that does not read as a credible greenhouse or cultivation crown;
- containment communicated mostly by a yellow floor field rather than by a real isolation architecture.
