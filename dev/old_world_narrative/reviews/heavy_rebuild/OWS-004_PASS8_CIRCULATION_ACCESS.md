# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 8 — Circulation and Access

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Circulation hierarchy

OWS-004 must support four distinguishable movement systems:

1. public visitors;
2. production staff/player route;
3. freight/material/harvest flow;
4. maintenance and dirty/spent-substrate service flow.

They may intersect at controlled transfer points but cannot collapse into one generic stair hall.

## Public route

`front forecourt -> public/demo lobby -> controlled viewing/demo zone -> return/exit`.

The public route stays in the lower podium and does not pass through receiving, substrate return, packing or production service aisles. A protected view into cultivation technology may reveal the system without becoming an uncontrolled production entrance.

## Staff/player vertical route

A continuous usable stair/landing route must connect:

`podium staff transition -> cultivation 1 -> cultivation 2 -> cultivation 3 -> upper cultivation/containment-capable floor -> roof/service crown`.

Critical requirements:

- no one-block squeeze points at primary landings;
- no machinery blocking stairs or doors;
- clear two-block headroom;
- legible landing connection to each production module;
- later containment barriers on the upper floor must not make the canonical evidence route impossible.

## Freight and harvest movement

The east service spine provides the architectural abstraction of a freight/material lift and vertical transfer. Each production floor requires a staging/handoff zone adjacent to it.

Inbound route:

`receiving -> clean/substrate/culture staging -> freight core -> production-floor handoff`.

Outbound harvest route:

`production-floor handoff -> freight core -> harvest consolidation -> grading/packing -> rear dispatch`.

This route should not require produce or pallets to cross the visitor lobby.

## Dirty / spent substrate route

Spent biological material and sanitation return must have a service-side route back toward a separated dirty-return zone. It should avoid clean intake and public circulation as much as the Minecraft plan permits.

## Maintenance route

Maintenance access connects the service spine to environmental branches and the roof plant. The crown needs a walkable route around major plant masses and a controlled way into the greenhouse/showcase volume.

## Egress

The west/secondary core provides an alternate protected vertical movement/egress mass. It should have landings aligned to the principal production levels rather than reproduce the donor's exposed decorative stair flights.

## Access acceptance

Gate B fails if the player cannot traverse the intended staff route without breaking blocks, if freight/harvest logic has no vertical transfer, if receiving and dispatch collapse into one threshold, or if public circulation passes through dirty/service zones.
