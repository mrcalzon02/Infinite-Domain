**Status:** scale study complete; no Pass-6 geometry authored

## Scale decision

The baseline is large enough to say “garage” but too compressed to separate three cell functions, a technician spine, customer/service control, parts issue, calibration records and exterior recovery/rework grounds. The study envelope expands by eight blocks in X, three in Y and eight in Z while remaining a compact common roadside site.

The 49 x 18 x 41 bound is a ceiling for the first Gate-A study, not an automatic final entitlement. It is materially smaller than regional Atlas halls and must not become a factory campus.

## Minecraft movement dimensions

- Vehicle/service thresholds: five blocks wide and five blocks clear high per cell, with structure above acting as real frames.
- Customer and staff doors: two blocks high; primary customer approach at least three blocks wide externally.
- Technician spine: four blocks deep in plan, preserving at least two blocks of clear walking width after utility/service placement.
- Transverse movement field: four blocks deep and free of permanent equipment below Y4.
- Operator approaches: two blocks clear in front of guarded machinery.
- Maintenance access: one block minimum around working machine faces; no inaccessible prop backs.
- Customer/service room: minimum three-block clear internal depth after desk/glazing.
- Proof/records route: minimum two-block-high approach and a clear block above the eventual deterministic container.
- Roof plant: at least two-block service clearance around major units and a future connected access route.

## Vertical hierarchy

- Y0: site/drainage datum.
- Y1: finished floors/aprons.
- Y2–6: normal player, desk, vehicle and machine operating zone.
- Y7–9: customer/support roofs and framed service-door heads.
- Y10–13: clear-span hall structure and overhead handling/service rails.
- Y13–16: attached roof monitor/utility volumes.
- Y14–17: Atlas blade and high-point identity, subordinate to overall 18-block bound.

The height increase is functional: it permits service-door framing, overhead handling and connected roof utilities. It must not create a hollow upper storey.

## Scale checks against role

- The envelope still reads as a common roadside depot, not OWS-010's transfer hall or OWS-013's assembly hall.
- Three bays remain the dominant frontage motif inherited from the donor.
- Support space occupies a compact side bar rather than doubling the building.
- Exterior recovery, delivery and rework grounds are present without consuming a campus-scale lot.
- The proof/records zone is large enough to be credible but cannot become a lore vault.
- No crisis damage, encounters, loot placement or final proof coordinate is introduced by this scale study.
