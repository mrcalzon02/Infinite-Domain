## Actual institution

OWS-002 is a **municipal community center rapidly converted into a civil emergency food-security production and relief-distribution facility using Verdant Continuum Foods Evercrop systems**.

It is not a normal vertical farm, not a VCF research site, not a supermarket, not a permanent warehouse, and not an evacuation shelter with decorative crops. Its architectural identity comes from the collision of two readable systems:

1. the retained civic building and public-access logic of the pre-crisis community center; and
2. a standardized emergency cultivation/relief program imposed quickly but competently by municipal authorities and VCF.

The intact state must communicate that this conversion worked. The tragedy is that officials continued operating it under food-security pressure after one local biological/material warning emerged.

## Primary mission

The facility receives VCF cultures and nutrient stock, grows food at unusually high throughput inside the commandeered multipurpose hall, checks and packs harvested output, and distributes food/culture kits to the surrounding public under emergency authorization.

The player should understand this mission from architecture before reading the proof item.

## Required public sequence

`street approach -> controlled queue / waiting -> registration -> emergency authorization -> culture-kit or relief issue -> controlled departure`

Public circulation must remain outside the active cultivation/service core except at deliberate handoff points. Registration and issue are adjacent but not identical functions. The front civic lobby remains the public threshold because the emergency conversion reuses a familiar community destination rather than hiding the operation in an industrial compound.

## Required operational sequence

`rear/service receiving -> count / condition / batch check -> clean culture and nutrient stock -> irrigation / nutrient service -> cultivation rows -> harvest transfer -> wash / harvest check -> packing -> pallet/crate staging -> outbound dispatch`

This is the main back-of-house production chain. It must be physically continuous and must not depend on walking freight through the public lobby.

## Required reverse / exception sequence

`grow rack or service anomaly -> local isolation -> staff inspection / quality decision -> marked hold position`

OWS-002 is an early-containment site. The exception path must remain local. One rack, feed loop, or service bay becomes suspect while the rest of the hall continues producing and dispatching food. The building must therefore contain ordinary isolation capacity before later historical overlays are applied.
