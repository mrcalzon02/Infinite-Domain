# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-002 Pass 15 — Encounter Architecture

**Target:** OWS-002 — Verdant Continuum Foods Emergency Community Grow Hall  
**Site scale:** common/medium neighborhood civic conversion  
**Encounter rule:** danger must follow the ruin; it may not rewrite the early-containment history.

## Encounter density

OWS-002 does not need a dungeon population. The final D3 should use at most **two vanilla spawners**, each tied to a distinct damaged ecological niche and neither positioned in the proof room or directly beside irreplaceable evidence.

## Encounter 1 — damaged grow-hall pressure

**Creature:** `minecraft:zombie`  
**Purpose:** modest threshold/interior pressure encounter after the player understands the public intake route.  
**Placement concept:** eastern/southern edge of the high grow hall near the long-abandoned suspect rack and wet service damage, but outside the central aisles and away from the proof container.

The player should be able to use rack banks, structural bays and service edges as cover/navigation. The spawner must not sit in the middle of a clean aisle as an obvious game object.

Recommended configuration: low count, limited nearby cap, appropriate to a common site.

## Encounter 2 — optional wet-service side risk

**Creature:** `minecraft:spider`  
**Purpose:** optional risk/reward pressure in the west wash/support wing where broken glazing, dark wet-service corners and long abandonment justify spiders/webs.  
**Placement concept:** deep west/rear wash-support corner, off the mandatory proof route.

This encounter may protect supplemental practical loot but never the unique proof item.

## Protected navigation

The final D3 must preserve:

- north public entrance and interior orientation route;
- at least two usable grow-hall aisles;
- the west harvest/wash connection;
- south relief-dispatch route as a recognizable alternate exterior connection;
- a straightforward, non-destructive route to the proof chest.

Spawner placement may create pressure around these routes, not physically block them.

## Safety around proof

No creepers or explosion-centric encounter design is authorized. No spawner is placed inside registration/authorization records. The proof chest remains reachable without breaking blocks and without entering an optional high-risk side room.

## Encounter rejection conditions

Reject if:

- more than two spawners are required to make the site interesting;
- monsters are scattered randomly without environmental justification;
- the player can lose the only proof through encounter placement;
- combat forces the building to behave like a dungeon instead of a ruined civic/production facility;
- D0 or D1 contains monster spawners.
