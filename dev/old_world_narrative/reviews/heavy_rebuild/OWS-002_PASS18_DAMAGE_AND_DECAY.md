## Primary failure chain

OWS-002 is abandoned while still fundamentally recognizable. Its long-term ruin is driven by exposure and failed building services:

1. roof-lantern glazing fails in several localized panels;
2. one lower-roof service penetration near the plant opens to weather;
3. water enters the west/rear support and high cultivation hall;
4. irrigation/service piping fails locally after maintenance stops;
5. one previously suspect cultivation-bank segment partially collapses and spills soil/debris;
6. wet floor areas become mossy/dirty and collect limited vegetation/webs;
7. receiving and dispatch weather at their outer edges while retaining usable openings;
8. abandoned relief stock remains at real staging points;
9. the north public/records zone remains comparatively protected, preserving deterministic proof acquisition.

### Public/records zone
