## Central transfer hall

Arrange four north-south lane modules in the high bay. Each contains input, drive, transfer, routing and output zones. Preserve a shared structural rhythm while differentiating the machinery clearances of standard crates, small components, heavy assemblies and overflow/exception work.

Required adjacencies:

- south induction crossfeed touches every lane start;
- north destination cross-transfer trunk receives every lane output;
- west operator gallery runs parallel to all four lanes;
- a guarded central cross aisle connects operator and maintenance sides without becoming freight path;
- east maintenance/service route reaches every drive and isolation point;
- overhead catwalk crosses or parallels the lane bank with two real stairs/landings where practical;
- a guarded service trench or depressed drive gallery connects the principal drive clusters;
- Lane 04 lies closest to parts/rework so later cannibalization and staged modules remain causal.

No principal route may require jumping a conveyor, passing under an unsafe one-block clearance or using a one-block ladder.

## North cross-transfer and outbound return

The north end of the lane bank must visibly merge into a common destination trunk. From there:

- accepted loads route east along the trunk;
- a protected east-side return/drop connection carries loads toward outbound accumulation;
- destination splits feed separate Dock 03 and Dock 04 buffers;
- one manual divert reaches rework without reversing through an active lane;
- control-room glazing overlooks the lane outputs and trunk;
- roof/upper structure reflects the transfer turn rather than remaining a generic monitor field.
