The original east/rear ladder/trapdoor location is preserved rather than moved merely to simplify the review model.

### 6. Adaptive-reuse layering — RESOLVED

The macro geometry now visibly separates three architectural generations without needing final signs:

1. retained low civic/support architecture;
2. retained tall hall and lantern;
3. targeted emergency/VCF additions at north intake, east receiving, south dispatch and roof service.
