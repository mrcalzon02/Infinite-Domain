## Historical rule

OWS-002 is not a late-collapse containment laboratory. Its tragedy is that an ordinary civic building was converted into a genuinely successful emergency food-production and relief node, then kept operating after a small warning signal appeared because local food security was more urgent than a single suspect cultivation segment.

The historical layers must remain visually separable.

## Layer 1 — Civic normality beneath the conversion

The surviving community-center architecture remains visible in the low west wing, public lobby, former kitchen/wet-service rooms, roof lantern and civic-scale site. This layer explains why the facility has a public entrance and neighborhood dimensions rather than warehouse-scale logistics.

## Layer 2 — Emergency food-security conversion

Civil authorities and VCF retrofit the building for Evercrop deployment:

- public registration and emergency authorization at the north entrance;
- culture/relief issue behind a controlled counter;
- high hall converted to repeated two-tier cultivation banks;
- nutrient/irrigation service added to the hall and roof plant;
- east receiving/batch check and clean culture stock;
- former kitchen converted to harvest wash/check;
- former stage/south hall zone converted to packing and relief dispatch.

This is a successful operational layer, not desperation.

## Layer 3 — Demand pressure

Demand rises while the grow system still works. D1 should show pressure at actual choke points:

- additional relief crates/pallets at south staging without blocking the dispatch lane;
- additional authorization/queue material near public intake;
- extra batch-check and clean-stock staging at east receiving;
- temporary yellow routing and priority markings that remain subordinate to permanent VCF/civic identity.

The building is busier, not yet broken.

## Layer 4 — Localized early anomaly

One cultivation/service segment becomes suspect. The problem must be geographically specific and operationally understandable.

Chosen anomaly: the eastern end of the third cultivation bank, nearest clean-stock/irrigation service, is isolated after repeated quality exceptions. Its associated floor/service zone receives temporary hold markings, replacement containers and a small inspection barrier. Adjacent banks continue operating.

The anomaly must never become "fungus everywhere." A player looking at D1 should understand that staff believed the problem was bounded and manageable.

## Layer 5 — Continued operation around the problem

Relief operations continue. The suspect segment is bypassed rather than allowing the whole hall to stop. Clean stock, wash/check, packing and dispatch remain in service. This is the core environmental story: the warning existed, but the institution continued because the cost of shutting down emergency food production was immediate and visible.

## Layer 6 — Abandonment and long decay

The site ultimately stops operating through infrastructure abandonment rather than a cinematic explosion. D3 damage follows a coherent chain:

1. maintenance and staffing cease;
2. roof-lantern glazing and roof-service penetrations fail;
3. rain/wind enters the high hall and west wet-service zone;
4. irrigation lines fail and leak locally;
5. exposed crop banks and service fittings rot/collapse;
6. south dispatch and east receiving remain recognizable but accumulate abandoned stock and weather damage;
7. long-term moss, dirt, webs and localized vegetation occupy wet/exposed areas;
8. structural frames, major circulation, proof access and enough cultivation infrastructure survive to reconstruct the facility's history.

## Non-negotiable historical constraints

- D0 stays identical to the passed Gate-B r2 operational interpretation.
- D1 may not contain major structural collapse.
- D1 anomaly remains limited to one rack/service segment.
- Public relief demand and the anomaly coexist; the building does not instantly become a quarantine fortress.
- D3 remains recognizable as the same facility.
- Damage follows roof/glazing/water/service failure, not random subtraction.
- The deterministic proof location remains safe and reachable.
