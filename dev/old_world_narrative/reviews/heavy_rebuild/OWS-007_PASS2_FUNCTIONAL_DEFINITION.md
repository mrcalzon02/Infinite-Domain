## Facility definition

OWS-007 is a commercial crop-development and durability-validation campus for EP-7. It is not a generic wet laboratory, seed bank, greenhouse or nuclear annex with agricultural props. It receives controlled EP-7 lots, establishes reference growth, exposes matched samples to environmental and distribution stresses, measures survival and food quality, reseeds recovered material, and records whether a trait is ready for commercial release.

The intact D0 institution must read as five connected systems:

1. **public and research threshold** — VCF reception, product-development interpretation, visitor observation and badge-controlled staff entry;
2. **accession and reference chain** — sample receiving, batch registration, clean/dry hold, baseline seed/culture archive and preparation;
3. **controlled trial chain** — reference cultivation, environmental stress, spore-survival and storage/transport ageing chambers;
4. **recovery and assessment chain** — phenotyping/imaging, germination and reseeding trials, harvest/sample handling and food-quality review;
5. **release and custody chain** — comparative data review, retained samples, commercial durability approval and secure records.

## Primary research workflow

The operating loop is:

`controlled lot receiving -> accession / batch identity -> baseline archive and preparation -> reference cultivation -> assigned stress or storage trial -> monitored sampling / phenotyping -> germination and reseeding verification -> food-quality comparison -> release decision -> retained sample and secure record`

Failed or inconclusive samples return to controlled hold or repeat testing. Approved samples move to retained stock and downstream distribution records. Waste, spent substrate and wash return use a separate service path.

## Required trial families

### Reference cultivation

A successful control environment establishes how an untreated EP-7 batch grows under intended commercial conditions. This must be visibly linked to every stress result; durability has no meaning without a control.

### Environmental stress phenotyping

Matched cultures experience controlled drought, heat, cold and salinity/nutrient interruption. Repeated cells should share service and measurement geometry while carrying distinct conditions. Imaging or scanning stations compare growth, recovery and yield rather than relying on decorative crop differences.

### Spore-survival testing

A more tightly bounded suite measures whether spores remain viable after drying, airborne exposure or surface persistence. It requires controlled access, separated service/air branches and a wash/return route, but the pre-crisis site must not be portrayed as an emergency quarantine facility.

### Storage and transport durability

The laboratory simulates commercial distribution conditions: cold hold, dry ageing, humidity cycling and repeated handling/transport stress. This sequence should connect the cooler/archive banks, environmental conditioning and retained package lots into one test system.

### Reseeding and food-quality release

Recovered material is germinated and replanted to prove persistence across another growth cycle. Successful plants then reach a food-quality/analysis node where yield, texture, composition or other release criteria are reviewed against the baseline. This is the point where VCF converts survival into a celebrated product claim.
