# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-002 Pass 11 — Operational Systems

**Target:** OWS-002 — Verdant Continuum Foods Emergency Community Grow Hall  
**Status:** COMPLETE AS D0 OPERATIONAL CONTRACT

## Operational premise

Every major system must answer a real work question: what arrives, where it is checked, how crops are supported, how harvest moves, where it is washed, how relief output is packed, and how the building is maintained.

## Cultivation banks

The grow hall uses repeated modular two-tier cultivation banks rather than three decorative crop strips.

Preferred lower-floor organization:

- bank A around X=24..26;
- primary aisle X=27..29;
- bank B around X=30..32;
- primary aisle X=33..35;
- bank C around X=36..38;
- east service strip X≈39..41;
- receiving/nutrient support at the far east wall.

Rows run broadly north-south through the production hall, with clear north and south cross-aisles.

Each rack should show:

- structural uprights;
- lower crop bed;
- upper crop bed/service tier;
- open aisle-facing sides;
- local irrigation branch;
- task lighting above/between tiers where appropriate;
- harvest access.

## Irrigation / nutrient service

The east service bay contains the nutrient and irrigation control point.

Visible system:

`stock / make-up position -> pump/manifold -> main overhead service line -> repeated branch lines to grow banks`

Use Create/pack blocks already accepted by the pack where available, including fluid pipes and mechanical pumps. Do not route main pipes through player head-height in guaranteed aisles.

The service network should visibly connect to the cultivation system instead of ending in decorative pipes.

## Environmental support

Controlled-environment agriculture also needs light/air/service support.

D0 should include:

- roof-lantern daylight contribution;
- repeated task/grow lighting under the hall frame;
- limited ventilation/service equipment cues from the roof plant;
- overhead service runs with maintenance space;
- no implication that the glazed lantern alone magically controls climate.

## Receiving / batch check

East receiving requires:

- incoming pallet/crate pocket;
- count/condition check bench;
- one transfer/depot point;
- clean stock immediately upstream;
- ordinary D0 hold/isolation location for uncertain material;
- records/labeling cues.

The normal hold location is the place later historical anomaly layers will stress. It is not itself a crisis in D0.

## Clean culture / nutrient stock

Use disciplined shelves/crates/barrels/cooler-like equipment only where they express specific stock types. Clean culture stock must remain distinct from nutrient/service inventory and from outbound packed relief goods.

## Harvest wash/check

Former kitchen/wet-service infrastructure supports:

- water line;
- wash basins/cauldrons;
- harvest check counter;
- drain/sanitation cues;
- transfer depots;
- task lighting.

Raw harvest enters from the hall, checked harvest exits toward packing.

## Packing

Packing uses:

- counters/work benches;
- package/crate materials;
- pallet build area;
- small side stacks of completed goods;
- clear central outbound lane.

The work area must look capable of throughput but not like a permanent industrial packaging factory.

## Relief dispatch

South dispatch requires:

- release/check point;
- staging pockets on either side of the central lane;
- south doors and apron;
- emergency routing floor marks;
- no fixed fixture in the guaranteed outbound route.

## Public issue

Culture-kit/relief issue at the north front uses small clean stock behind the counter and controlled staff replenishment. It is a handoff function, not a retail store.

## Communications / records

Emergency coordination requires at least:

- radio/communications point;
- allocation/authorization records desk;
- storage/shelving for paperwork or supplies;
- visually plausible proximity between public authorization and operational staff access.

## Roof service

The west/rear roof plant remains separated into multiple serviceable units. Visible pipes/conduit may connect toward the hall/wet-service functions, but roof equipment must not be sprinkled randomly across the lantern.

## D0 system checks

The intact review should fail if:

- crop banks have no service aisles;
- pumps/pipes do not connect to crops;
- receiving lacks inspection/hold capacity;
- clean stock and outbound relief staging are mixed;
- harvest wash has no water/service cues;
- packing blocks the dispatch lane;
- upper crop tier has no plausible support/access;
- roof plant has no relation to systems below;
- the building reads as crops + crates rather than an operating relief-production system.
