## What the proof establishes

OWS-004 is the primary physical proof that Evercrop-scale fungal agriculture was not a fringe experiment. It was engineered into a standardized, publicly presented, high-throughput urban production system.

The building should establish that claim before the player opens the chest:

- a public demonstration interface presents the process as normal civic/commercial infrastructure;
- four repeated cultivation modules show standardized industrial replication;
- water/nutrient and environmental systems operate vertically across the tower;
- clean receiving, spent return, harvest handling, grading, packing and dispatch form a complete production chain;
- the upper-floor containment history demonstrates that this ordinary food infrastructure later became part of the active-containment crisis.

The handbook confirms and names what the architecture already proves.

## Relationship to the upper containment story

The proof is intentionally **not** stored on the isolated upper floor. OWS-004's progression function is stronger when the player first acquires evidence of ordinary industrial cultivation and can then explore upward to discover how a system that had become normalized was later placed under containment pressure.

This creates the intended evidence sequence:

`ordinary public institution -> standardized production handbook -> repeated cultivation system -> upper containment history -> later systemic/maintenance failure evidence`.

## Combat independence

The deterministic proof is never a combat reward.

The optional D3 encounter pockets at the rear service edge and crown may create exploration pressure, but:

- neither is required to reach the proof chest;
- neither controls the front records room;
- no spawner may be placed directly adjacent to the proof route;
- the player must be able to acquire the handbook even if they avoid the optional upper/rear encounter pockets entirely.

## Future-system restraint

This pass implements only the current Old World exploration/proof role. It must not activate any later-return, Darknet or other future-phase mechanic unless that system separately names OWS-004 and provides an explicit implementation contract.
