# OWS-009 Pass 10 — Interior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-009 — Atlas Roadside Automated Repair Depot  
**Status:** implemented in Gate-B r1 candidate

The public bar contains a north waiting/check-in room and south service-consultation room behind a glazed controlled boundary. The parts band divides east receiving from west clean issue. The rear support band separates controlled records/proof adjacency from the south-facing core/rework buffer; the proof node is a locked records-side room and contains no proof container at Gate B.

Transparent safety wings bound the three work cells without interrupting vehicle movement or the transverse field. The hall remains operationally connected, while staff, customers, parts and removed cores cross only at explicit controlled thresholds.
