- Never place practical loot in the center of protected grow aisles.

- Do not stack dozens of containers merely to imply abundance; the production system itself establishes scale.
- Relief crates should accumulate at actual staging/choke points, especially in D1/D3.
- The proof container remains visually associated with records/authorization, not pallets or random crop racks.
