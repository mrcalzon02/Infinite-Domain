# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-003 Pass 15 — Encounter Architecture

**Target:** OWS-003 — Verdant Continuum Foods Cold-Chain Culture Nursery  
**Status:** COMPLETE — planning only until Gate-C implementation

## Encounter role

OWS-003 is primarily an evidence/logistics site, not a combat dungeon. The final ruin may contain limited vanilla hostile occupation, but combat must not become the mechanism that proves the story or unlocks the deterministic evidence.

## D0 / D1

No spawners. The intact and early-anomaly states represent operating infrastructure.

## D3

Use at most **two** deliberate vanilla spawner niches, both spatially justified by the abandoned building:

1. **Receiving / quality-hold service pocket** — one low-intensity zombie spawner in the degraded east service side, away from the main proof route.
2. **Roof-plant / rear maintenance pocket** — one spider spawner associated with the failed maintenance/roof-service zone or rear dispatch edge.

Exact mob choice may be adjusted during implementation if block geometry makes one position clearly more plausible, but:

- total deterministic spawners must remain 0–2;
- no spawner may sit in the front batch/licensing office;
- no spawner may occupy the proof chest’s opening volume;
- no spawner may be required to reach the proof;
- main circulation must permit bypass/retreat rather than trap the player in a single arena.

## Encounter readability

The final ruin should provide natural tension pockets through:

- broken sightlines at receiving;
- partial cold-room/glazing failure;
- maintenance tower verticality;
- crate/pallet cover near logistics edges;
- roof/wet-zone shadows.

Do not fill every room with hostiles or convert the nursery into a wave-combat facility.

## Pass 15 decision

**ENCOUNTER ARCHITECTURE: COMPLETE FOR GATE-C IMPLEMENTATION.**

OWS-003 remains a discovery/logistics site with optional localized danger, not a mandatory combat gate.
