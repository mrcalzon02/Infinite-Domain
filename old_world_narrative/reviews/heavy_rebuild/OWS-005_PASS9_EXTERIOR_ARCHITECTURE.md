## Freight thresholds

The near-twin Gate-A canopies now have different operating identities.

- **Receiving:** open framed raw-harvest bays, lot lanes, yellow vehicle/transfer markings, washable trench drainage, brick donor-memory surfaces and direct access to raw staging.
- **Dispatch:** insulated cold-side dock seals, paired finished-lot doors, polished transfer floor, cyan cold-chain framing, separate trench drainage and direct adjacency to cold hold.

The two assemblies share the accepted corporate structural rhythm without implying the same cleanliness or product state.

## Public face

The QA pavilion gains a paved approach, recessed double entrance, supported canopy, room-aligned front/side glazing and a protected process-overlook relationship. Its white/glass material language remains visibly cleaner and lower than the industrial halls.

## Production elevations and roof

VCF white/lime/cyan panels are restricted to the sanitary, clean-pack and cold-chain zones they identify. Structural piers, high clerestory glazing, monitor frames, roof gutters, downpipes and connected plant break the broad elevations without changing accepted masses.

## Donor memory

Brick remains concentrated around raw receiving. It reads as inherited industrial fabric absorbed into a newer VCF sanitary system, not as the dominant facility identity.
