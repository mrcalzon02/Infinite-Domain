## Exterior visual findings

Blocking findings:

1. **The building does not read as a pilot laboratory.** It is a dark, broad clinic/office box with white roof slabs and sparse windows.
2. **VCF identity is applied color.** Green/white bands do not create controlled-environment or bioscience architecture.
3. **The principal entrance is weak.** The thin canopy does not establish visitor reception, badge control, sample intake or staff access.
4. **No laboratory service anatomy is visible.** There is no credible exhaust hierarchy, air-handling plant, utility spine, cylinder/reagent delivery, wash/decon threshold or controlled waste edge.
5. **The roof is forgotten.** Large flat roof fields contain no plant sized or arranged to support three culture chambers and a materials-observation suite.
6. **Facade openings do not explain the rooms behind them.** Sparse cyan windows and long blank walls remain donor conventions rather than program-driven laboratory elevations.
7. **Receiving and waste routes are absent.** The site does not distinguish clean sample/reagent intake from outgoing contaminated material or general servicing.
8. **The building lacks a memorable research identity.** Nothing in the silhouette communicates paired-culture research, monitored environmental chambers or a pilot process.

## Interior / sectional findings

The floor slices expose three glass rectangles inserted into a large cleared donor room. Their equal treatment communicates repetition but not a scientific comparison sequence. There are no credible chamber vestibules, operator galleries, controlled access points, pass-through/sample transfer, wash/decontamination support or connected environmental services.

The polymer-observation area reads as disconnected plastic and scaffolding masses. It does not explain how coupons are prepared, exposed, observed, retained or compared to controls. The proof chest is mechanically deterministic but visually located within the cleared chamber field instead of a secure records or principal-investigator custody point.

Much of the remaining donor interior is either hidden behind the monolithic shell or uncommitted to a necessary room. The structure therefore fails the room-legitimacy, operational-realism, circulation and hero-space standards even though static lint passes.
