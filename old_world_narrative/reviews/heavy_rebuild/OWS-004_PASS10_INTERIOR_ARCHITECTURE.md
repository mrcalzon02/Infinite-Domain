- entry/orientation zone;
- demonstration/viewing space with controlled visual access to cultivation technology;
- staff transition/control threshold;
- records/handbook location appropriate to an operational public-production institution;
- physical separation from receiving, dirty return and packing traffic.

## Industrial podium

Receiving and packing/service spaces need real rooms or bounded zones:

- conditioned/clean inbound staging;
- clean stock/culture support;
- freight-core handoff;
- harvest consolidation;
- grading/inspection;
- packing;
- outbound staging;
- separated spent-substrate/dirty-return service area;
- utilities and maintenance support.

## Cultivation modules

Each of four floors requires a coherent module with:

- controlled entry vestibule or threshold;
- principal three-block service aisle where possible;
- cultivation-bank zones on both sides or equivalent balanced arrangement;
- branch service wall/riser edge;
- inspection/work point;
- harvest/freight handoff;
- sanitation support;
- distinct floor cues sufficient for navigation without making four unrelated designs.

The modules should be standardized enough to communicate industrial replication.

## Player readability
