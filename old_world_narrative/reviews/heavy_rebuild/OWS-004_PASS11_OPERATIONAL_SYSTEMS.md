# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 11 — Operational Systems

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Operating model

OWS-004 is a controlled-environment biological production tower. Its machinery and utility props must form connected systems rather than isolated visual decoration.

## Environmental control

Each cultivation floor requires recognizable environmental supply/return infrastructure tied to the east service spine and roof plant. The system should communicate:

- fresh-air / conditioned-air distribution;
- humidity/environmental management;
- return/exhaust path;
- branch points at each production floor;
- an independently isolatable branch for the upper cultivation floor.

Create/IE/TFMG blocks may abstract real ducts, fans and manifolds, but their physical routing must remain coherent.

## Water / nutrient / culture support

A vertical water/nutrient service header should run through the service spine, with floor-level branches to cultivation banks and local tanks/manifolds. The rooftop crown/plant provides a visually credible header/storage origin.

## Cultivation equipment

Each production module should contain dense but navigable grow banks, using fungal/mycelial substrates and appropriate support framing. Equipment density must preserve the protected main service aisle and freight handoff.

Floor modules also require inspection/work surfaces and local service points rather than only crop blocks.

## Material logistics

Inbound clean stock/substrate/cultures stage at receiving and transfer into the freight core. Harvest returns through the freight/service system to podium grading/packing. Spent biological material follows a separated dirty-return route.

Use crates, pallets, depots and industrial storage in amounts that explain throughput without turning every aisle into clutter.

## Harvest and packing

The podium must contain an intelligible harvest chain:

`harvest receipt -> inspection/grading -> packing stock -> finished-product staging -> dispatch`.

This should be spatially distinct from inbound receiving.

## Sanitation / contamination control

Controlled thresholds, wash/sanitation points and dirty-return separation should communicate that contamination was an ordinary operational concern even before the later crisis.

## Roof plant

The roof requires connected environmental equipment, service piping/duct abstraction, maintenance clearance and a real tie into the vertical spine. The greenhouse/showcase volume should have its own cultivation/environmental connection rather than simply sharing the roof visually.

## Operational guardrails

Gate B fails if:

- environmental equipment has no connection to the riser or floor branches;
- grow banks block required circulation;
- receiving cannot reach the freight core;
- harvest cannot reach packing/dispatch;
- dirty return crosses the public/demo path unnecessarily;
- the upper production floor cannot be isolated through pre-existing branch controls;
- roof equipment is decorative and disconnected.

**OPERATIONAL SYSTEMS: COMPLETE.**
