# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-006 Pass 10 — Interior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-006 — Verdant Continuum Foods PT-9 Symbiosis Pilot Laboratory  
**Status:** COMPLETE FOR GATE-B R1 REVIEW  

## Public and observation program

The front bar is divided into visitor interpretation, central reception and staff/administrative support. Glazed internal boundaries expose the research institution without allowing the public route to open directly into controlled workrooms.

The transverse hero space is a single observation/control gallery with a continuous floor datum, chamber-aligned control zones and unobstructed views toward A/B/C.

## West preparation wing

The west wing is divided in workflow order:

`sample/reagent receipt -> accession and cold hold -> media/reagent preparation -> wash/decon -> rear service return`

Double internal doors and a continuous service aisle make the sequence usable rather than diagrammatic.

## Chamber interiors

Overlapping study-shell boundaries are replaced by reinforced, continuous separation walls. Each chamber receives a hygienic floor, controlled public-side entry, rear maintenance entry and protected center aisle.

The chamber volumes remain deliberately unequal: A is a restrained reference room, B is the largest symbiosis trial, and C is a divided stress/recovery room.

## East analysis and records wing

The taller east wing is divided into:

- bacterial reference/control work;
- clean polymer-coupon preparation;
- isolated exposed-material observation;
- principal-investigator review;
- secure records reservation;
- upper data/records mezzanine connected to the maintenance stair.

## Rear service spine

The rear is divided into plant control, shared environmental manifold and controlled waste/service holding while maintaining a through service route.

No significant interior is left without an operating purpose.
