# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 4 — Program and Adjacency

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Program hierarchy

The 51 x 47 x 47 tower will be organized as a vertically stacked production institution with a public/industrial podium, repeated controlled cultivation floors, a separated upper containment-capable floor and a greenhouse/environmental crown.

The goal is to make vertical logistics understandable from both exterior form and interior exploration.

## Podium / ground program

The base must carry the heaviest logistics and public separation. Required zones:

- public forecourt and VCF demonstration entrance;
- visitor orientation / cultivation demonstration and protected viewing threshold;
- staff-control transition;
- service receiving with pallet/substrate/culture staging;
- clean stock / batch intake;
- dirty-return / spent-substrate staging separated from clean intake;
- freight/material lift lobby;
- harvest receiving / consolidation;
- grading/check and packing;
- outbound dispatch;
- utility/service rooms tied to the production risers.

Public approach and outbound freight should not be the same threshold. Clean inbound stock and dirty biological return should likewise avoid one ambiguous shared pile.

## Vertical core

The core should read as a deliberately serviced agricultural tower rather than office circulation. It requires:

- principal staff/player stair route;
- emergency/secondary egress where practical;
- freight/material lift volume represented architecturally even if Minecraft mechanics abstract the actual lift;
- vertical water/nutrient/service header;
- environmental air supply/return spine;
- harvest/dirty-return connection back to the podium;
- maintenance access continuing to the roof plant.

The service spine should be visible enough from selected exterior elevations to contribute to the tower identity.

## Cultivation floors 1–3

The lower three main cultivation floors remain successful in D0 and still productive when the upper floor enters containment history.

Each floor should contain:

- controlled entry from the core;
- two or more cultivation banks/rack fields;
- protected principal service aisle;
- perimeter or dedicated environmental distribution;
- local nutrient/water/service manifold;
- batch inspection/work surface;
- harvest handoff adjacent to the freight/service core;
- sanitation/cleaning support;
- windows/clerestories or facade expression aligned to production bays rather than office cubicles.

Repeated modules should be similar enough to read as industrial standardization but varied enough that floor navigation is not four cloned rooms.

## Upper cultivation / future containment floor

The highest primary production floor must work normally in D0 but have pre-existing architecture that later allows isolation:

- controlled vestibule/doors at core entry;
- separable environmental branch;
- visible shutoff/service station;
- local inspection/quality-hold area;
- a service route that can later be sealed without blocking all tower access;
- stronger boundary construction than ordinary open office partitions.

D1/active-containment history can then add seals, rerouting, warning fields and stock isolation without inventing a quarantine architecture from nothing.

## Roof / crown

The crown must combine cultivation/showcase and environmental plant into a recognizable tower cap:

- glazed greenhouse or luminous controlled cultivation volume;
- air-handling/exhaust stacks and service equipment;
- nutrient/water header tanks or equivalent storage forms;
- maintenance walkway/access from the core;
- visible relationship to the main service riser;
- strong VCF identity appropriate to a flagship urban technology site.

It should be taller/more architecturally intentional than the baseline roof box while remaining subordinate to the overall tower height.

## Exterior adjacency expression

The outside should let a player infer where functions are:

- **public/front:** transparent, cleaner, optimistic lower facade and demonstration threshold;
- **service/receiving:** heavier dock/service architecture at a different side;
- **production tower:** repeated cultivation bands / controlled glazing / structural bay rhythm;
- **service spine:** vertical utility or maintenance expression breaking the office-box uniformity;
- **dispatch:** distinct lower loading/packing edge;
- **roof:** greenhouse/environmental crown tied to the riser.

## Exploration route

A strong player path should support:

`public/demo -> staff transition -> lower production floor -> repeated tower ascent -> upper containment evidence -> roof/service crown`,

while alternate service routes expose receiving, freight, harvest and packing systems.

The canonical handbook should be obtainable through a credible records/demonstration/production-supervision node without requiring the player to defeat optional spawners or break the structure.

## Adjacency constraints for later gates

Gate A must prove the podium, cultivation-floor hierarchy, service spine and roof crown without relying on racks or signs.

Gate B must prove all major routes and operational connections before damage.

Gate C may transform the upper floor into active containment history and later D3 ruin, but it may not destroy the legibility of the lower productive system or evidence route.
