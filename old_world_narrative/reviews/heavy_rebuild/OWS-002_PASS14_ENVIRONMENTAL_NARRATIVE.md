# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-002 Pass 14 — Environmental Narrative

**Target:** OWS-002 — Verdant Continuum Foods Emergency Community Grow Hall  
**Narrative fact to communicate without text:** civil authorities deliberately deployed VCF Evercrop as emergency food security, saw a localized quality problem, and continued relief production around it.

## Required player-readable story

A player entering the ruin should be able to infer the broad sequence before opening a book or proof item:

1. this was a public/civic place;
2. VCF converted it into a serious cultivation facility;
3. food was issued to the public here at scale;
4. one grow/service segment was treated differently from the rest;
5. the rest of the hall continued operating;
6. the final ruin came later through abandonment and infrastructure decay.

## Environmental evidence by zone

### North public intake

Preserve the strongest civic-facing route. Registration/authorization furniture, public queue spine, culture/relief issue counter and professional VCF/emergency signage establish that this was a sanctioned public program rather than scavenger agriculture.

D1 demand pressure appears as additional authorization material and relief containers near—but not inside—the clear queue route.

### Grow hall

Three repeated two-tier banks, structural portal frames, clerestories and connected irrigation are the dominant evidence of organized production. The third/eastern bank receives the one localized anomaly overlay. The other banks remain visually normal in D1, making the decision to continue operations legible.

### East receiving and quality control

Batch-check counter, clean culture stock, nutrient service and ordinary D0 quality hold establish that inspection existed before the crisis. D1 adds a specific suspect-lot transfer toward the third-bank isolation area, showing an institution responding through normal quality systems before broader collapse.

### West wash/check

The former civic kitchen is visibly repurposed for harvest washing and checking. This makes the process chain understandable: crop production did not terminate at decorative plants; harvested material moved into sanitation/inspection before packing.

### South relief dispatch

The clear yellow handoff lane, packing benches, staged crates/pallets and external dispatch portal demonstrate throughput. D1 increases backlog/relief staging at the sides of the lane while keeping the route usable.

### Roof/service layer

The lower-roof plant visibly connects to the hall irrigation trunk. D3 water ingress and pipe/glazing failure should cluster along these systems, so the ruin itself explains how weather and abandoned maintenance destroyed a once-coherent facility.

## D1 anomaly language

The localized suspect segment should use:

- temporary yellow floor/service marking;
- a small framed inspection/isolation barrier around only part of the third rack;
- one replacement/failed stock cluster;
- direct operational signage such as `RACK 3 / QUALITY HOLD` or `INSPECT BEFORE HARVEST`;
- no generalized red quarantine field;
- no broad fungal carpet;
- no major collapse.

## D3 environmental language

Long-term damage should expose the D1 layer rather than erase it:

- broken lantern panes over the hall;
- localized roof/service opening and water staining beneath;
- failed/removed irrigation pipe segments near the suspect rack;
- moss/coarse dirt where water collected;
- a partially collapsed rack segment with soil/debris beneath it;
- abandoned relief stock at receiving/dispatch choke points;
- limited webs/vegetation in wet corners;
- surviving signs, structural frames and healthy-bank remnants sufficient to reconstruct normal operation.

## Narrative rejection conditions

Reject the pass if:

- the player must read the proof to understand that food production/relief happened here;
- all grow banks look equally contaminated in D1;
- D3 is simply D0 with random holes;
- the quality-hold language implies the site began as a containment facility;
- relief staging blocks the intended player/operational route;
- damage destroys the public-to-hall or proof route without a deliberate alternate path.
