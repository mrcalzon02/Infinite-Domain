## Scale decision

**Retain the existing `51 x 21 x 47` template envelope for the first Gate-A study.**

No expansion is currently justified. The donor already has enough volume for a medium civic emergency facility: a distinct lobby, broad lower civic wing, high-volume hall, wet-service rear band, two service/egress thresholds, roof lantern and maintenance access.

The problem is not insufficient size. The problem is that the present internal and exterior hierarchy does not express emergency cultivation and relief logistics strongly enough.

Expansion remains available if Gate A proves the converted massing cannot support a convincing dispatch or service relationship, but the doctrine does not require enlargement for its own sake.

## Minecraft one-meter translation rules

The build must compensate for Minecraft's coarse grid by exaggerating circulation and service clearances where a real facility would rely on centimeters rather than meters.

### Public circulation

- principal public queue lane: **minimum 3 blocks clear**;
- lobby circulation between queue, registration and issue: **minimum 3 blocks clear** at primary route;
- individual counter approach: **minimum 2 blocks clear**;
- public entrance/exit thresholds: retain paired/double-door reading where possible;
- queue geometry must not reduce the road-facing lobby to a one-block maze.

### Staff/service circulation

- primary staff production route: **minimum 3 blocks clear**;
- secondary service aisle: **minimum 2 blocks clear**;
- freight/pallet handoff at receiving and dispatch: **minimum 4 blocks clear** around the principal threshold where template bounds permit;
- roof-maintenance approach: minimum **2 blocks clear** at ladder landing/hatch.

### Cultivation geometry

The current shipping overlay uses long two-block crop strips at only three Z positions. The rebuild must instead read as repeated serviceable bays.

For the retained hall volume:

- cultivation rack/bay width: normally **3–4 blocks** including structural edge/service hardware;
- primary aisle between rack banks: **3 blocks clear**;
- local secondary access between modules: **2 blocks clear** where justified;
- rack length: approximately **10–16 blocks**, broken into modules rather than one uninterrupted decorative strip;
- two-tier cultivation may use lower and upper crop levels, but each tier requires believable working clearance and must not collide visually with the roof lantern;
- overhead irrigation/lighting/service zone should occupy a distinct elevation above the normal player aisle rather than passing through head height.

The hall must remain visually open enough that the roof lantern and civic-origin volume are still legible.

## Vertical scale

The donor hall reaches approximately Y=18 with the lantern extending to the top of the `21`-block template.

This is appropriate for:

- lower cultivation tier;
- upper cultivation tier or raised service framing;
- overhead irrigation/lighting/service runs;
- visible roof structure/daylight volume;
- maintenance access above the production floor.

Do not fill the hall vertically with solid rack walls. The intact building should still read as a converted civic hall, not a warehouse compressed into a cube.
