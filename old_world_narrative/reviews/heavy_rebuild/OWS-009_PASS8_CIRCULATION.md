# OWS-009 Pass 8 — Circulation and Access

**Target:** OWS-009 — Atlas Roadside Automated Repair Depot  
**Status:** implemented in Gate-B r1 candidate

Five route families are explicit and protected:

1. three continuous north-to-south vehicle axes connect the recovery apron to diagnostics, heavy repair and calibration/release;
2. the four-block transverse movement field remains clear across all cells;
3. the four-block rear technician spine remains clear beneath overhead services;
4. customers enter independently through the east public bar and reach a controlled service-handoff door without entering a vehicle cell;
5. east parts receiving/issue and south core/rework return use opposed external thresholds and separate controlled internal routes.

Nine two-wide iron-door assemblies regulate customer, hall, parts, records and core crossings. Floor datums distinguish public white/cyan, service orange, hazardous yellow and technician black/factory routes without narrowing playable clearances.
