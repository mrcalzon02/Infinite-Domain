# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 12 — VCF Institutional Identity

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Identity objective

OWS-004 is one of VCF's most visible public statements: industrial fungal agriculture presented as clean, advanced, dependable urban infrastructure. Its institutional identity must therefore be stronger and more optimistic than the utilitarian cold-chain facilities while remaining consistent with VCF's broader green/white language.

## Architectural identity

VCF is communicated through:

- clean white public/demo architecture against heavier industrial service construction;
- lime cultivation/service bands aligned to actual production modules;
- controlled cyan/light-blue glazing/logistics cues where appropriate;
- greenhouse crown as a visible symbol of continuous urban cultivation;
- repeated standardized production bays that imply corporate replication and scale;
- clear separation between polished public presentation and hidden service intensity.

## Wayfinding hierarchy

Purposeful signs should reinforce existing architecture rather than create it. Required sign families include:

- primary VCF / facility identity at public approach;
- visitor/demo guidance;
- production-floor numbering/batch zones;
- clean receiving vs dirty return;
- freight/service core;
- harvest/packing/dispatch;
- environmental/maintenance access;
- upper-floor controlled access capable of later containment overlay.

## Public optimism

The intact D0 public zone should feel confident rather than defensive. Framed glazing, clean demonstration space and a visible cultivation technology showcase communicate that Evercrop was sold as progress, convenience and food security.

This optimistic layer is historically important because later active containment will visibly overwrite it rather than replacing an already ominous laboratory aesthetic.

## Production identity

Inside production floors, VCF identity comes from standardized floor layouts, numbered cultivation modules, consistent service manifolds, batch-control work points and repeated safety/cleanliness zoning. Every floor should look like part of the same operating system.

## Containment-ready identity

The upper floor's intact signage and zone coding should be normal production-control language. Later Gate C can overlay yellow isolation fields, temporary seals, rerouting and emergency instructions. The player should be able to distinguish permanent VCF architecture from later containment intervention.

## Evidence identity

The `kubejs:evercrop_cultivation_handbook` belongs naturally at a public demonstration / production-supervision records point where training and standardized cultivation instructions would be used. The 01a/01b audio material remains associated deterministically with this evidence path.

## Restraint

Do not blanket the tower in lime concrete. The VCF identity should remain legible if all text were removed: public/demo transparency, production-module rhythm, service anatomy and greenhouse crown must carry most of the institutional story.
