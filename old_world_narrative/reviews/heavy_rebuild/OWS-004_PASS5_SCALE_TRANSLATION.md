# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 5 — Minecraft Scale Translation

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE FOR GATE-A STUDY

## Scale decision

Retain the current **51 x 47 x 47** envelope for the first Gate-A massing study.

The donor already provides sufficient height and plan area for a convincing podium, repeated cultivation floors, service core/spine and roof crown. Expanding before proving hierarchy would add blocks rather than solve the baseline problem. The failure is architectural organization and expression, not insufficient gross volume.

Any later size increase requires a specific functional blocker discovered in Gate A/B; it is not pre-authorized.

## Vertical stack translation

The 47-block height should read as a deliberate agricultural stack rather than twenty office-like floor bands. The massing study should consolidate visual hierarchy into approximately:

- **Y 1–10:** public/industrial podium and logistics base;
- **Y 10–15:** transition/mechanical/service band as needed;
- **four principal cultivation levels** occupying the middle/upper tower with enough clear height for grow banks, service aisles and environmental distribution;
- **upper containment-capable production level** expressed as the highest repeated cultivation module rather than an arbitrary yellow room;
- **roof/crown:** greenhouse plus environmental/nutrient plant and maintenance access, kept within the existing overall envelope where practical.

Exact floor elevations may shift during implementation, but the facade should visually group the building into production modules rather than preserve the baseline office-window cadence.

## Podium translation

The lower tower should use roughly the existing 51 x 47 site footprint but avoid one monolithic black slab.

Gate-A massing should reserve distinct architectural volumes for:

- public/demo frontage;
- service receiving and clean intake;
- freight/core base;
- harvest/packing and outbound dispatch;
- utility/service rooms.

These need not become separate buildings. Stepped setbacks, canopies, service courts and differentiated height can make them legible within the retained footprint.

## Production floor clearances

A Minecraft cultivation module needs more vertical space than a real-world floor represented literally at one block per meter because racks, service pipes, player headroom and environmental equipment all compete for the same volume.

Use a target clear interior height of approximately **5–6 blocks** for primary cultivation modules, with structure/service bands between modules where possible. This supports:

- 2–3 block-high grow banks;
- minimum two-block player clearance;
- overhead/perimeter services;
- visible environmental distribution;
- controlled doors and cross-aisles.

Do not compress a cultivation level into office-ceiling proportions just to preserve donor slabs.

## Circulation widths

For later Gate B:

- principal cultivation/service aisle: target **3 blocks clear** where the plan permits;
- secondary rack aisles: generally **2 blocks clear**;
- receiving/freight and harvest/packing movement: target **3–4 blocks clear** at critical transfer points;
- public route: minimum **2 blocks clear**, widening at demonstration/viewing zones;
- stairs/core landings: never rely on one-block squeeze points;
- containment floor approach: protected route must remain usable after later isolation elements are added.

Gate A must reserve enough massing/core area for these routes even though it will not yet populate racks.

## Service spine translation

The vertical agricultural utility system must occupy a meaningful width. Reserve an identifiable riser/service band approximately **3–5 blocks wide** through the tower, paired with maintenance access where feasible. It should have a visible roof connection and floor branches later.

A one-block decorative pipe column is not sufficient to represent the tower's environmental, water/nutrient and material-service dependency.

## Roof crown translation

The roof greenhouse/environmental crown should be a recognizable architectural object, not a tiny box. Within the 51 x 47 roof plan, reserve:

- a glazed cultivation/showcase volume large enough to read in oblique views;
- adjacent environmental plant masses;
- service/walkway space around equipment;
- a direct connection to the riser/core.

The crown should materially improve the silhouette while staying proportional to a 47-block tower.

## Gate-A scope

The first massing model may establish only:

- podium stepping and public/service hierarchy;
- main tower production-module proportions;
- structural/facade bay rhythm at macro scale;
- service-spine mass;
- greenhouse/environmental crown;
- major receiving and dispatch thresholds as architectural openings/volumes;
- primary vertical-core reservation.

It must **not** use mushroom racks, machinery density, proof chests, containment damage, spawners, signage clutter or microdetail to compensate for weak massing.

## Gate-A acceptance question

From the fixed exterior cameras and roof/top review, before interior production detail exists, does the 51 x 47 x 47 model read as an advanced urban controlled-environment agricultural tower with a public/industrial base, repeated production modules, a major service spine and greenhouse/environmental crown—rather than as an office tower recolored green?

If not, revise massing before Gate B.
