## Facility definition

OWS-008 is an emergency fungal-persistence investigation and sterilization-verification laboratory operated by Verdant Continuum Foods during active containment. It is not a normal product-development lab, a generic glass-box clean room or a mountain biohazard donor with VCF colors. It receives incident specimens and failed clean-zone components from other VCF sites, preserves chain of custody, compares matched controls, applies independently monitored treatment cycles, verifies apparent clearance, challenges treated assemblies under controlled conditions, then opens service walls and joints to find where contamination persisted.

The intact D0 institution is already in emergency operation. It must read as a competent commercial bioscience facility converted into an incident laboratory by successive professional interventions, not as a final ruin and not as improvised panic from the outset.

The building must express six connected systems:

1. **incident threshold and command** — controlled arrival, security, case registration, response briefing and facility status;
2. **specimen custody and dirty examination** — sealed receipt, accession, retained controls, initial microscopy/inspection and dirty-side preparation;
3. **treatment and transfer** — separately monitored wash, heat/steam, chemical/sterilant and air/filter interventions with material pass-throughs;
4. **clean hold and verification** — post-treatment quarantine, surface checks, environmental sampling and time-separated release decisions;
5. **persistence challenge and service-joint investigation** — controlled recurrence testing, exposed wall/floor penetrations, destructive joint inspection and comparison against apparently negative room surfaces;
6. **plant, waste and records** — segregated air/wash/sterilant/drainage systems, treated-waste hold, incident analysis and secure chronology.

## Primary investigation workflow

The canonical operating chain is:

`secure incident receipt -> case registration / chain of custody -> dirty examination and matched-control split -> assigned treatment cycle -> monitored material pass-through -> clean hold -> immediate verification -> timed persistence challenge -> service-joint inspection -> comparative analysis -> incident decision and secure chronology`

An apparently negative surface result does not end the process. Material remains in clean hold, moves into timed challenge, and is inspected at seams, pipe penetrations, drain edges, panel backs and other concealed interfaces. Positive joint samples return to dirty-side analysis under a new case revision; they do not reverse through the clean route.

## Required treatment and comparison families

### Dirty-side accession and retained controls

Incoming specimen containers, removed wall/service components and swabs require a sealed receiving threshold, case identity, a dirty examination bench and a protected retained-control store. The reference material must remain visibly connected to later treatment results so the facility reads as an investigation rather than four unrelated contamination rooms.

### Wash and chemical sterilization

One branch tests wash pressure, cleaning chemistry and sterilant contact. It needs visible supply, drainage, tool return and a monitored cycle boundary. The architecture must distinguish delivery and dirty drainage from clean-side transfer.

### Heat / steam treatment

A second branch tests thermal treatment of tools, panels or sealed sample assemblies. It needs a thicker chamber/plant relationship, service access and a pass-through logic rather than a decorative furnace in a laboratory.

### Air and filter intervention

A third branch investigates whether pressure, filtered air and duct replacement restore a zone. Its supply and exhaust path must be traceable to roof/rear plant, and the service cavity behind the room must remain independently accessible.

### Clean hold and delayed verification

Treated material enters numbered clean holds where staff record immediate negatives, wait, resample and compare environmental readings. Holds should share a disciplined architectural family while differing in treatment feed and inspection condition. They must not become identical glass cubes with colored stripes.

### Persistence challenge and joint forensics

The hero investigation deliberately recreates a cleaned room segment around wall/floor joints, utility penetrations and removable panels. Staff can observe the clean face, then enter a separate service gallery to open the concealed side. This is where the structure proves its central fact without depending on a book: exposed room surfaces read clean while contamination reappears behind a seal or service penetration.

## People, specimen, clean-material and waste circulation

Five routes must remain distinguishable:

- **incident visitor / command:** north public threshold -> security -> briefing/status room -> protected observation -> exit;
- **laboratory staff:** badge threshold -> lockers/change -> staged personnel decontamination -> dirty or clean work assignment -> separate return/change sequence;
- **specimen/material:** east secure receipt -> accession -> dirty examination -> treatment -> one-way pass-through -> clean hold -> challenge/joint inspection -> retained sample or record;
- **waste/dirty return:** dirty rooms and opened service cavities -> kill/wash point -> treated-waste hold -> rear/east removal, never through clean hold or the lobby;
- **maintenance:** south/rear service threshold -> plant spine -> chamber backs, service-joint gallery and roof systems without crossing public circulation or the one-way clean transfer.

Observers and incident command must be able to compare rooms without entering active test cells. Positive findings return through a dirty-side transfer or sealed pass box, not by carrying material backward through the clean corridor.
