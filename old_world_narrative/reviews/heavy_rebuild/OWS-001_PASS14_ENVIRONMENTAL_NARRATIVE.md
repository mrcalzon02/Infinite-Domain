# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-001 — Pass 14 Environmental Narrative

## Narrative question

What can the player infer **before reading a single lore item**?

OWS-001 must communicate that Verdant Continuum Foods operated an ordinary neighborhood biological-food culture service, that returned/reusable material was part of the normal economy, and that the first warning appeared as a mundane quality/cold-chain problem rather than an obvious apocalypse.

## Evidence clusters

### Public pavilion

The entrance, corporate identity, dual handoff counters and intact wayfinding establish normal daily use. This area should remain comparatively orderly even in D3. The player should understand that ordinary residents came here routinely rather than visiting a restricted research facility.

### Culture issue / cold lockers

Repeated refrigerated banks and the clean-side connection to cold holding establish that the commodity being issued required dependable cold-chain service. D1 should show one localized service exception without making every locker fail at once.

### Return sanitation

Reusable return crates, wash infrastructure and inspection surfaces establish a circular service economy. This is the strongest physical place to show the first strange material problem: replacement seals, rejected liners, damp/softened container interfaces and unusually frequent inspection.

### Quality hold

D0 contains modest normal quality-hold capacity. D1 expands the pressure on that space rather than inventing a new quarantine wing. By D3 the player should still be able to read that workers were sorting suspect returns instead of simply abandoning the first anomaly they saw.

### Receiving / batch check

Inbound freight, staging pockets and the batch/temperature station show normal logistics. D1 exception paperwork/marking belongs here because the problem is becoming measurable across shipments.

### Supervisor / batch records

This is the narrative anchor and the correct place for deterministic proof. It is not a treasure vault. It is where ordinary management and batch-control records would plausibly survive.

### Roof cold plant

Rooftop refrigeration and the east service riser explain the cold rooms below. D3 roof failure should visibly affect the parts of the interior that depend on this plant; damage is strongest when the player can infer cause and effect.

## Environmental storytelling rule

Every unusual block or damage cluster must answer one of three questions:

1. What did this room do in normal operation?
2. What changed during the early anomaly?
3. What failed only after centuries of abandonment?

If it answers none of them, it is decorative noise and should not be added.
