**Status:** COMPLETE

## Facility definition

OWS-004 is a high-rise controlled-environment mycological production building. It is not merely a farm placed vertically, and it is not a laboratory. Its reason for verticality is to stack repeated environmental production modules above a dense urban logistics/support base while sharing centralized water, nutrient/substrate, environmental-control, freight and maintenance systems.

The finished D0 institution must be understandable as three interlocking systems:

1. **public/commercial face** — visitor demonstration, VCF education/branding and proof that Evercrop was publicly normalized;
2. **production tower** — repeated controlled cultivation floors with visible floor-to-floor environmental and material services;
3. **industrial base/core** — receiving, clean stock/substrate support, freight transfer, harvest consolidation, packing, dispatch, utilities and maintenance.

## Primary material workflow

The intended material flow is:

`service receiving -> clean/substrate/culture intake -> vertical freight transfer -> controlled cultivation floors -> harvest transfer -> grading/check -> packing -> outbound dispatch`

Supporting service flow:

`water/nutrient/environmental plant -> vertical risers -> floor distribution -> drain/return/waste handling -> maintenance/service plant`.

Public flow:

`public entry -> demonstration/interpretation -> controlled viewing threshold -> exit`,

without crossing dirty receiving, production servicing, harvest return or containment circulation.

## Cultivation-floor logic

The four production floors are not identical decorative mushroom rooms. Each is a repeated controlled module with:

- grow banks/racks and protected service aisles;
- environmental supply/return points for temperature, humidity and fresh-air control;
- water/nutrient or culture-support service points appropriate to Evercrop's fictional industrial biology;
- local inspection/work bench;
- material-lift connection;
- harvest-transfer point;
- sanitation/cleaning support;
- clear separation between principal production circulation and equipment/service edges.

The repetition should be architecturally visible from outside through bay rhythm, clerestories/glazing, service bands or other production-specific expression.

## Vertical logistics

A credible tower requires at least two distinguishable vertical systems:

- **people/egress core:** safe principal vertical movement and emergency egress;
- **production/service spine:** freight/material transfer plus environmental/nutrient utilities.

These may share a larger core volume but cannot collapse into one unexplained staircase.

Harvest must move down toward packing/dispatch without forcing the player to imagine produce being hand-carried down office stairs. Receiving stock must likewise have an understandable route upward.

## Public demonstration function

The lower podium should present Evercrop as a triumph of urban food security. The public-facing zone therefore needs:

- an intentional public entrance rather than a service door;
- a demonstration or viewing zone that reveals cultivation technology without exposing production routes;
- optimistic VCF architectural language layered over the industrial base;
- physical separation from receiving, dirty returns and staff/service circulation.

This is important narrative evidence: society celebrated and normalized the system before it understood the later risk.

## Rooftop function

The roof/crown must operate as a real environmental and cultivation layer, not a decorative green box. It should combine some subset of:

- controlled greenhouse/cultivation demonstration space;
- environmental air-handling/exhaust equipment;
- nutrient/water storage or header distribution;
- maintenance walkways and protected access;
- visible connection to the vertical production-service spine.

## Containment history requirement

The D0 building is successful and ordinary. Later history must be able to isolate the upper cultivation floor while lower floors remain operational.

Therefore the intact architecture must already provide believable controlled boundaries at that floor: doors, service isolation, independent or branchable environmental supply/return, and a route that can later be sealed without inventing a new building system after the anomaly appears.
