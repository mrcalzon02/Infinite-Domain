# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-002 Pass 10 — Interior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-002 — Verdant Continuum Foods Emergency Community Grow Hall  
**Status:** COMPLETE AS D0 INTERIOR CONTRACT

## Interior design premise

The D0 interior should make the emergency conversion legible before any historical damage is applied. Former civic rooms are repurposed with disciplined temporary/permanent insertions rather than erased into one industrial volume.

## Public lobby / waiting

The projecting north lobby remains the main public orientation space.

Required features:

- clear double-door arrival;
- durable civic floor retained beneath emergency routing;
- three-block principal queue path;
- limited benches/standing guidance rather than retail shelving;
- emergency information board / sign wall;
- visibility toward registration and issue points;
- no direct uncontrolled path into the crop hall.

## Registration / authorization

Use the north public/administrative bar for:

- registration workstations;
- emergency authorization/allocation desk;
- records shelves/communications;
- deterministic proof location reserved for the final historical state;
- staff-side access behind public counters.

Counters must have a public side and a staff side. The proof station should feel like the place where authorizations and allocation decisions are recorded, not a random loot corner.

## Culture-kit / relief issue

The east side of the north public bar becomes issue/handover.

Required features:

- issue counter with cleanable top;
- small staff-side culture-kit stock;
- direct controlled staff replenishment route toward clean stock/grow operations;
- clear public handoff without allowing recipients into production.

## Staff coordination / support

Remaining west/north civic rooms become compact staff functions:

- emergency coordination/radio desk;
- packaging/tool storage;
- staff rest/basic support;
- retained washroom/sanitation where useful;
- maintenance storage.

At least one former-civic architectural trace may remain in finishes or room proportion, but no room should still function as a library/craft/childcare space.

## Grow hall

The hall remains visually tall and open despite dense production.

Required interior hierarchy:

- high exposed hall structure;
- roof lantern visible from the main lower floor;
- modular cultivation banks rather than continuous opaque walls;
- two three-block primary aisles;
- north and south cross-aisles;
- east grow-service strip;
- upper rack/service tier below primary roof structure;
- overhead irrigation/lighting arranged from structural frames;
- no clutter in guaranteed travel volumes.

The player should be able to stand in a main aisle and understand the entire cultivation system at a glance.

## East receiving / clean stock

Receiving is a distinct room/zone inside the east threshold:

- batch/condition check bench;
- incoming crate/pallet pocket;
- clean culture/nutrient stock immediately upstream of the grow hall;
- normal D0 quality-hold/isolation position;
- nearby nutrient/irrigation service bay.

The room should not look like a generic warehouse because volumes remain civic-scale.

## Harvest wash / check

The former community kitchen/wet-service area becomes:

- wash basins/cauldrons;
- drain/water service cues;
- harvest check counter;
- depot/transfer points;
- washable work surfaces;
- limited clean/dirty separation around raw versus checked output.

The former kitchen geometry is deliberately reused because it already explains water and service infrastructure.

## Packing / relief staging

The former south stage becomes a practical processing platform/zone:

- packing counters;
- package/crate preparation;
- checked-goods transfer from wash;
- pallet/crate staging at side pockets;
- broad center dispatch lane to south doors;
- visible emergency-yellow floor routing.

Do not preserve auditorium seating in the active D0 program.

## Floors

Use floor materials to communicate process without turning the building into colored stripes:

- public: polished/neutral civic surface;
- staff/admin: neutral durable floor;
- grow hall: cleanable stone/factory-floor service paths with agricultural beds as equipment;
- wet harvest: cleanable/washable surface;
- packing/dispatch: durable factory-floor/asphalt transition;
- quality hold: localized yellow boundary only.

## Ceilings and lighting

Public/admin:

- lower civic ceiling feel;
- regular ceiling lights;
- limited exposed retrofit conduit.

Grow hall:

- high exposed framing;
- repeated overhead service/lighting lines;
- lantern daylight remains visible;
- no decorative ceiling that hides service logic.

Wet/packing zones:

- task lighting over work surfaces;
- visible utility runs appropriate to conversion.

## Pass-10 rejection conditions

Reject D0 if:

- former civic rooms merely keep their old furniture with new signs;
- counters lack public/staff sides;
- crop racks eliminate hall openness;
- receiving, wash and packing feel interchangeable;
- roof lantern becomes visually irrelevant inside;
- dispatch lane is filled with props;
- interior function cannot be inferred without reading every sign.
