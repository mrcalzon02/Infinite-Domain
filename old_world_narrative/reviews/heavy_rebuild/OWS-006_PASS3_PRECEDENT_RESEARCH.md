## NIH laboratory planning modules

The NIH Design Requirements Manual treats the laboratory/support module as a repeated planning unit that regularizes work area and building services. The useful lesson for OWS-006 is that repeatable experiments need repeatable spatial/service bays, while shared support functions remain deliberately adjacent rather than scattered as props.

Architectural implications:

- three comparative chambers should share a coherent structural and service rhythm;
- each chamber needs equivalent observation, sampling and utility access so comparisons appear operationally credible;
- preparation, support and analysis rooms should be planned as adjacent modules around a controlled circulation spine;
- overhead/roof service distribution should connect to the chamber grid and remain accessible for maintenance.

Reference: https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/NIH/NIHDesignManual.pdf

## CDC/NIH microbiological laboratory guidance

The CDC/NIH *Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories* guidance emphasizes risk-based controls, restricted laboratory access, handwashing near exit, cleanable work surfaces, separation of containment equipment from airflow disruption and planned mechanical ventilation/directional airflow. OWS-006 is not being assigned a real biosafety certification, but these relationships make its early professional laboratory believable.

Architectural implications:

- controlled doors and staff thresholds must precede active culture rooms;
- handwash/decon and cleanable preparation zones belong at laboratory exits and service transitions;
- chamber workstations cannot sit in a busy public corridor;
- air supply/exhaust, filtration and monitoring should be legible building systems;
- the later failure story can exploit compromised interfaces and filters precisely because those barriers existed in D0.

Reference: https://www.cdc.gov/labs/bmbl/index.html

## Cornell greenhouses and growth chambers

Cornell's research growth facilities use dedicated growth chambers to isolate experiments and control light, temperature, humidity and, in some chambers, carbon dioxide. The facilities pair specialized chambers with staff support and centralized environmental control rather than treating each chamber as a freestanding glass box.

Architectural implications:

- the three trial chambers need visible environmental feeds, monitoring and isolation;
- chambers may have distinct test conditions while sharing an operator gallery and service plant;
- the symbiosis showcase should visibly differ from reference and stress/recovery trials;
- roof/mechanical plant, sensor consoles and maintenance access are part of the experiment;
- VCF's optimistic public display can use controlled glazing without making the active chambers directly public-accessible.

Reference: https://cals.cornell.edu/agricultural-experiment-station/greenhouses-growth-chambers

## Boyce Thompson Institute plant-growth facility

The Boyce Thompson Institute describes a facility that combines greenhouses, walk-in and reach-in growth chambers, a dew room, an isolated plant-pathogen room and a seed-harvest room. The relevant lesson is program diversity around controlled growth: preparation, specialized environmental rooms and downstream handling make the chambers useful.

Architectural implications:

- OWS-006 needs more than its three primary chambers;
- a humidity/dew or recovery-support zone can explain the stress trial;
- isolated bacterial/polymer observation belongs beside, but not inside, the main cultivation sequence;
- downstream sample handling and records close the research loop;
- staff and facility-support spaces should occupy the donor's side wings instead of leaving large generic voids.

Reference: https://bti.cornell.edu/facilities/plant-growth-facility/

## Precedent synthesis for Minecraft

The four governing relationships are:

- **repeatable chamber modules + shared services** instead of unrelated glass boxes;
- **restricted clean-to-controlled progression** instead of lobby-to-lab direct access;

- **preparation -> trial -> sampling -> analysis -> secure record** instead of room chains without material logic.
