# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-003 Pass 9 — Exterior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-003 — Verdant Continuum Foods Cold-Chain Culture Nursery  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Exterior design objective

The final intact exterior must communicate **an older agricultural processing plant converted by VCF into controlled biological cold-chain infrastructure**. Corporate identity should come from later technical insertions, clean thresholds, cold-service architecture and batch logistics—not a continuous green stripe.

## North/front administrative face

Character:

- retained brick/masonry cannery annex;
- later white clean-zone entrance projection;
- constrained framed glazing at staff/batch-control rooms;
- restrained lime VCF identity concentrated at the authorized entrance;
- supported canopy sized to the doorway rather than the entire facade.

The front should look organized and corporate, but not retail-facing.

Required final identity positions:

- full `VERDANT CONTINUUM FOODS` name;
- `COLD-CHAIN CULTURE NURSERY` facility identity;
- staff/authorized access language.

## East receiving face

Character:

- most technical inbound facade;
- reinforced/service jambs and steel header;
- recessed clean receiving vestibule;
- light-blue/cyan routing mark reserved for temperature-controlled logistics;
- inspection glazing where staff need to see dock activity;
- loading apron with real pallet handoff positions;
- utility/service penetrations tied to receiving hold and refrigeration.

The receiving side should look like a cold-chain transfer point, not an ordinary warehouse dock.

## South/rear dispatch face

Character:

- distinct from inbound receiving;
- larger conditioned outbound staging relationship;
- clear dispatch door/frame and weather canopy;
- marked destination lanes;
- restrained yellow route marking at the handoff zone;
- shipping racks/pallet positions at sides of the lane.

The rear face should visually support the global distribution story.

## West/orchard face

Character:

- strongest retained donor history;
- older brick wall and orchard/service relationship;
- fewer clean VCF insertions than east/front sides;
- selective high service windows or vents corresponding to conditioned rooms;
- maintenance/service penetrations only where justified.

The orchard side should make the building's previous agricultural use visible beneath the conversion.

## High hall facade rhythm

Large walls should use the Gate-A 5–7 block structural rhythm.

Use:

- brick infill;
- visible concrete/steel pilasters;
- high clerestory or controlled observation openings where interior use requires them;
- insulated white service insertions at selected bays;
- vents/technical penetrations tied to cold-room or refrigeration functions.

Do not break walls randomly merely to create texture.

## Roof architecture

The roof must retain:

- three structured roof-light strips;
- a separated refrigeration plant field;
- maintenance walkways/gaps;
- service risers into the hall;
- access tower/landing;
- selective screening where needed without hiding every piece of equipment.

The roof should visibly belong to a facility maintaining a large conditioned volume.

## Material hierarchy

Older donor layer:

- brick;
- darker industrial flooring/service material;
- older concrete/stone roof edges;
- steel structural frame.

VCF conversion layer:

- white/light-gray clean insulated surfaces;
- restrained lime corporate markers;
- light-blue cold-chain routing accents;
- framed glass at monitoring/controlled thresholds;
- modern sheet-metal plant.

Operational/emergency routing:

- yellow only at hold/dispatch/hazard decisions, not as general decoration.

## Exterior anti-patterns

Do not allow:

- full-facade lime stripes replacing architecture;
- evenly spaced decorative windows unrelated to rooms;
- canopies floating away from doors;
- roof equipment with no service path;
- identical inbound and outbound portals;
- orchard foliage hiding the building again;
- random vents/pipes used to simulate complexity.

## Pass 9 decision

**EXTERIOR ARCHITECTURE: COMPLETE.**
