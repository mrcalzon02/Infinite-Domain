# OWS-010 Pass 18 — Damage and Decay

**Target:** OWS-010 — Atlas Conveyor Transfer Hall  
**Status:** implemented for Gate-C r1

Damage is localized to the system already weakened by D1 cannibalization. A small Lane-04 monitor opening retains its primary steel rails and weathered supported edges; fragments fall onto the locked lane directly below. A limited east clerestory/service-edge failure sheds material onto the exterior service strip rather than floating or crossing the building.

Water staining, moss, corrosion, cobwebs and restrained vegetation follow the monitor opening, east drain and service trench. The paired dock crowns, three maintained lanes, destination trunk, outbound return, operator gallery, catwalk, both circulation cores, control/records approach, utilities and Atlas identity remain readable. No generalized shell perforation or decorative random rubble is used.
