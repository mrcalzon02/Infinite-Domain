## Structural strategy

The accepted Gate-A masses are retained and resolved as one low-rise laboratory frame rather than overlapping unsupported shells.

- the public bar uses a repeated light-gray institutional bay grid supporting the deep canopy and roof edge;
- the transverse observation gallery uses four steel frame lines aligned to the chamber family;
- each of the three chamber volumes receives front/rear columns, intermediate frames and roof beams sized to its accepted height;
- the three chamber-monitor loads align with the chamber frames below;
- west preparation and east analysis wings use shorter repeated frames tied into the chamber/service grid;
- the rear service spine uses five major frames plus a continuous plant deck;
- branch plenums and the east maintenance core bear on the rear plant frame rather than floating above roofs.

Roof drainage follows actual volume edges at the public bar, chamber family, rear service spine and maintenance core. It is not used as decorative pipe noise.

## Gate-A preservation

Structural work does not change the accepted envelope, stepped composition, chamber hierarchy, wing heights, public entrance mass, gallery lantern, rear spine or environmental-plant skyline.
