# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-001 — Pass 16 Loot Architecture

## Loot hierarchy

Loot must follow the building's original use rather than being scattered to reward every room equally.

### Primary evidence / records

The **supervisor and batch-record station** is the authoritative evidence location. The deterministic Old World evidence chest belongs here because this is where batch manifests, service records and exception paperwork would plausibly be retained.

### Clean cold-chain stock

The east clean-storage and culture-locker spaces may contain sparse surviving service/food-production materials, sealed containers or maintenance consumables appropriate to cold-chain operations. These are secondary exploration rewards, not substitutes for proof.

### Receiving / crate consolidation

Freight and reusable-crate areas may contain low-value logistics supplies, containers and industrial odds-and-ends. Loot density must remain lower than the physical prop density so the depot does not feel like a treasure warehouse.

### Sanitation / quality hold

Any recoverable materials should be utilitarian cleaning, maintenance or rejected-container supplies. This is not a medical laboratory and should not produce unrelated high-tier biotech loot.

## Rules

- The guaranteed proof container must remain reachable without parkour or destructive excavation.
- No primary proof item may rely on random loot-table chance.
- Do not place valuable unrelated loot in the public lobby merely because it is easy for the player to find.
- Secondary loot must never obscure the operational route or the environmental evidence cluster that explains the room.
- D3 damage may expose or partly surround storage, but may not delete the deterministic proof location.
