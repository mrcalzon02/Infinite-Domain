## D3 blocking findings

D3 is mechanically plausible but visually underdeveloped for the centuries-later authoritative ruin state.

1. **The exterior silhouette remains too close to D0/D1.** In front-left and rear-right fixed cameras, the tower still reads primarily as a clean intact industrial high-rise. The upper-facade losses are too small and too visually quiet to communicate centuries of abandonment at normal review scale.

2. **The environmental crown remains overwhelmingly pristine.** The cyan greenhouse/crown is still one dominant uninterrupted roof volume from the exterior and top views. The roof/top projection contains only small localized cuts. Pass 18 explicitly requires the crown to be the strongest decay zone; r2 does not communicate that strongly enough.

3. **Upper service/environmental failure is not legible from the building exterior.** The causal history says the upper environmental system and containment layer fail first and hardest. r2 contains internal/local changes, but the fixed exterior views do not visibly explain that failure chain.

4. **The vertical decay gradient is too subtle.** Floor slices show some third/fourth-floor deterioration, but the difference between D1 and D3 is still modest relative to the building scale. The intended read—recoverable industrial farm below, failed containment and prolonged exposure above—is not strong enough yet.

5. **Long-abandonment material consequences are too sparse.** D3 needs more coherent upper-floor rack loss, wet/failed service zones, glazing/crown openings and localized facade/service breaches. This must remain causal and concentrated, not random checkerboard ruin.

## Required r3/r4 correction direction

The next visual revision must **freeze D0 and D1** and strengthen D3 only. Required emphasis:

- break the crown into visibly surviving and failed greenhouse/environmental zones rather than a nearly continuous cyan cap;
- introduce larger but structurally coherent crown/upper-level glazing losses visible from multiple fixed cameras;
- expose or remove selected upper environmental equipment/service runs so the failure can be read externally;
- increase fourth-floor grow-bank collapse/spill and localized third-floor wet/service damage while leaving lower floors comparatively legible;
- add one or two deliberate upper facade/service penetrations tied to the failed containment/environmental system;
- preserve the accepted east staff stair, east freight/material core, rerouted environmental-riser history, west secondary egress, primary VCF identity, proof route and deterministic proof chest;
- preserve exactly two optional D3 encounter spawners unless later encounter review explicitly changes that contract.

The next revision must not achieve visual difference by deleting random facade blocks or uniformly aging every floor. The failure gradient must remain top-heavy and operationally causal.
