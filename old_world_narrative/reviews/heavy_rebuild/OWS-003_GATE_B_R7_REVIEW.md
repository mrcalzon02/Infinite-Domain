## What r7 proves

The intact building now reads as a regional VCF biological cold-chain nursery installed into retained agricultural-industrial cannery bones rather than as a generic cannery with a small laboratory insert.

The exterior hierarchy is coherent:

- the high brick former processing hall remains the dominant adaptive-reuse mass;
- the orchard survives as older agricultural site history without hiding the building;
- receiving and outbound loading thresholds are visibly different operations;
- the rooftop refrigeration plant is large enough to explain the conditioned interior and is tied to a real maintenance tower/service route;
- roof-light strips and the repeated hall structure remain aligned with the industrial conversion;
- VCF white/lime/light-blue conversion language is subordinate to the retained brick shell rather than becoming a palette flood.

The interior/cutaway and floor slices support a reconstructable operating sequence:

`receiving -> intake/condition check -> pre-cool/hold -> cold vault / dormancy nursery -> release inspection -> conditioned packing -> outbound cold staging -> dispatch`

Batch registration, licensing/routing control and ordinary quality hold are separate support functions rather than decorative stations.
