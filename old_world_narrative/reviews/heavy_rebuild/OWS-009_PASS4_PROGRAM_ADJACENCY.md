# OWS-009 Pass 4 — Program and Adjacency

**Target:** OWS-009 — Atlas Roadside Automated Repair Depot  
**Orientation:** roadside/service frontage north; parts/rework service edge east; waste/core collection south-east  
**Status:** complete for scale translation; no geometry authored

## Program sequence

The plan is organized as a compact three-cell hall with a side support bar and rear technician spine:

`north recovery apron -> Bay 01 diagnosis/lockout -> transverse transfer aisle -> Bay 02 exchange/repair -> Bay 03 calibration/release -> north/east outbound handoff`

Parallel support:

`east parts delivery -> receive/secure stock -> parts issue -> rear technician spine -> three bays -> removed-core/rework hold -> south-east collection`

Customer path:

`north-east pedestrian entrance -> service authorization desk -> protected observation/status point -> release desk -> exterior`

| Customer/service bar | pedestrian entrance, Bay-01 observation, release records | active bay floor, parts delivery, waste/core hold |

| Utilities/plant | rear spine and overhead service rails | customer frontage |

## Circulation contracts

- Three vehicle openings remain visible from the north recovery apron.
- A four-block-deep transverse movement field remains clear inside the operational hall.
- The rear technician/service spine remains at least two blocks clear at walking height.
- Each machine cell retains at least one full block of service clearance on working sides and a two-block operator approach.
- Customers cannot step directly from the service bar onto an active lane.
- Parts move from the east threshold to issue without crossing the customer room.
- Removed cores/waste move to the south-east collection point without crossing clean parts or calibration storage.
- The proof/records node remains reachable by a deliberate staff/records route and will have clear space above its eventual container.
- Roof-plant access must not rely on decorative ladders leading nowhere; exact vertical circulation is deferred to Pass 8.

## Operational identities by cell

- recovery alignment datum;
- scan/inspection arch;
- restraint and lockout field;
- service authorization/status visibility;
- fault record transfer to the protected records node.

### Cell 02 — MODULE EXCHANGE / REPAIR

- lift or service pit;
- attached overhead handler;
- guarded press/assembly station;
- strip bench and tool supply;
- shortest route to parts issue and removed-core hold.

### Cell 03 — CALIBRATION / RELEASE

- repeatable alignment/load datum;
- calibrator access without passing through dirty rework;
- release inspection point;
- service plate/maintenance record custody adjacency;
- controlled outbound threshold.
