## Facility definition

OWS-005 is a regional harvest receiving, sanitary quality-control, packing and cold-dispatch plant. It is not a cannery, cultivation hall or generic warehouse. Its role begins when cultivation sites deliver harvested Evercrop and ends when coded finished cases leave under refrigeration.

The D0 institution must read as five coupled systems:

1. **raw logistics:** covered receiving, lot identification, weigh/check and dirty/raw staging;
2. **wet sanitation:** wash, PT-9 treatment, drainage, chemical/service support and staff hygiene control;
3. **quality and packing:** inspection, reject diversion, grading, case forming/filling, sealing and batch coding;
4. **cold chain:** precooling/finished cold hold, refrigeration plant and insulated dispatch threshold;
5. **institutional assurance:** a clean public/QA frontage presenting VCF's sanitary process as routine consumer protection.

## Material and people flows

Primary product flow is strictly one direction:

`west/raw receiving -> lot check -> wash/PT-9 sanitation -> inspection and reject diversion -> grading -> clean packing -> cold hold -> east/finished dispatch`

Clean packaging enters near the packing zone without crossing raw produce. Culled or suspect product exits through a controlled waste/reject route. Staff enter through change/hygiene control and can reach QA, sanitation and packing without using truck thresholds. Maintenance reaches refrigeration and roof plant from a service edge.
