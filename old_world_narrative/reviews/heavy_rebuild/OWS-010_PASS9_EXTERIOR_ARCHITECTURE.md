# OWS-010 Pass 9 — Exterior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-010 — Atlas Conveyor Transfer Hall  
**Status:** implemented in Gate-B r1 candidate

The passed r2 exterior remains authoritative: distinct inbound and outbound paired crowns, lane/truss-aligned piers, recessed gallery and clerestory bands, hall-facing control lantern, east service spine and process-indexed roof composition are unchanged.

Gate-B adds only program-linked intact infrastructure: staff/dock/service threshold lamps, pair-indexed dock bollards, south/east court drains and six frame-aligned roof downpipes. These additions reinforce actual movements and occupied rooms without enlarging the footprint or adding unrelated facade decoration.
