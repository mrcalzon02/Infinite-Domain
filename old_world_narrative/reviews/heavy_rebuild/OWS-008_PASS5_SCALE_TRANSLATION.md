## Scale decision

Retain **55 x 22 x 49** as the maximum envelope for the first Gate-A massing study. Do not preserve the donor's occupied volume block-for-block.

The baseline already fits public/security intake, two-level research, pressure-zoned containment, service rooms, rear plant and multiple exits. Its failure is that generic donor massing and repeated glass boxes do not express the investigation. Gate A should redistribute and carve the existing volume before requesting any worldgen expansion.

Any enlargement requires a demonstrated functional blocker and coordinator authorization. In particular, no expansion is justified merely to make larger decorative clean rooms.

## Plan translation

Use approximate planning bands rather than exact final coordinates:

- **north 7–10 blocks deep:** incident command, security, briefing and controlled staff threshold;
- **west 16–22 blocks wide:** two-level custody, examination, analysis, records and staff support;
- **east/central 24–32 blocks wide:** treatment modules, clean holds, observation and persistence challenge;

## Treatment module translation

Each principal treatment cell should be large enough to show a method and its boundary:

- target clear plan approximately **6–8 blocks wide by 7–10 blocks deep**;
- target clear height approximately **5 blocks**;
- **2-block clear** dirty operator/service clearance;

- a **2 x 2 minimum** material vestibule or pass-through expression where the process changes sides;
- 1–2 blocks of boundary/service depth through wall layering, recesses or back-of-cell plant;
- independent supply/drain/exhaust or thermal-service expression appropriate to the method.

Four modules may share a structural rhythm, but they should not be cloned. If all four treatment families cannot fit honestly, combine one method into a later revision cell rather than shrinking every room into a prop box.

## Clean hold and challenge translation

- clear plan approximately **5–7 by 6–8 blocks**;

- at least **2 blocks clear** around the retained/sample assembly where practical;
- visible sampling/record position outside the primary material path;
- no single door serving both dirty return and clean release.

Reserve a principal persistence-challenge / joint-forensics volume approximately **10–14 blocks wide by 12–18 blocks deep**, with **6–8 blocks clear height** where the stepped section permits. The room face, service wall and observation level must fit in the same section so the core contradiction is visible from a useful camera and player sightline.
