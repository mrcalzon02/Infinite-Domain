# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 15 — Encounter Architecture

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE — planning only until Gate-C implementation  
**Gate-B authority:** r4 PASSED

## Encounter role

OWS-004 is a vertical infrastructure/evidence site first. Its danger should come from exploring a tall abandoned production building with broken sightlines, isolated service pockets and a weather-damaged crown—not from converting every cultivation floor into a combat arena.

The active-containment history is also not represented by monsters in D1. Personnel were still operating the lower tower and attempting to isolate Cultivation 04.

## D0 / D1

**No deterministic spawners.**

D0 is normal commercial operation. D1 is an active institutional containment response while Cultivation Floors 1–3 and logistics remain productive. Neither state should contain abandoned-building hostile occupation.

## D3 deliberate encounter budget

Use at most **two** deliberate vanilla hostile spawner niches in the final ruin.

### Encounter pocket A — crown / failed greenhouse-service edge

Preferred mob: **spider**.

Place one low-intensity spider spawner in a degraded roof-greenhouse or environmental-maintenance pocket where failed glazing, overhead framing and partially sheltered plant equipment naturally create a later occupation niche.

Requirements:

- keep it away from the principal staff-stair landing and roof-access door;
- do not place it directly inside the environmental-riser repair route;
- preserve a retreat path back toward the staff core;
- do not require defeating it to understand the upper containment history.

### Encounter pocket B — rear dirty-return / dispatch service edge

Preferred mob: **zombie**.

Place at most one zombie spawner in the final degraded service/logistics side—preferably near spent-return or rear dispatch where exposed loading thresholds, abandoned crates and broken sightlines plausibly produce a later hostile pocket.

Requirements:

- keep it out of the public/staff-control evidence area;
- do not put it in the principal outbound aisle or immediately against an exterior entrance;
- preserve a bypass/retreat route through the podium;
- do not block the deterministic proof chest or its opening volume.

## Production-floor combat restraint

Cultivation Floors 1–4 should not each receive a spawner. Their repeated architecture is valuable precisely because the player can compare ordinary production floors with the historically isolated upper module.

Ambient danger may emerge naturally from world conditions, but deliberate encounter architecture should not obscure:

- the central cultivation aisles;
- the staff stair;
- the east freight/service core;
- the upper containment vestibule;
- the environmental branch-isolation evidence;
- the lower floors' continued-production story.

## Natural tension without spawner saturation

Use architecture to create exploration pressure through:

- long cultivation aisles with intermittent rack cover;
- partially failed glazing and cross-floor sightlines;
- stair/core verticality;
- the contrast between bright public spaces and darker service/return zones;
- crown framing and failed greenhouse panels;
- crate/pallet cover near loading edges;
- localized wet-service damage around the upper floor and environmental riser.

These conditions create uncertainty without turning the tower into a mandatory wave-combat dungeon.

## Protected zones

No deliberate spawner may occupy or directly control:

- the front public/staff-control records zone;
- the canonical proof chest opening volume;
- the principal staff stair or any required landing;
- the west secondary-egress ladder;
- the Cultivation 04 containment-control vestibule;
- the only traversable connection between two required narrative zones.

## Pass 15 decision

**ENCOUNTER ARCHITECTURE: COMPLETE FOR GATE-C IMPLEMENTATION.**

OWS-004 may contain two localized vanilla-hostile occupation pockets in D3, but its exploration and proof remain independent of combat. The tower stays a readable piece of failed urban food infrastructure rather than becoming a monster dungeon.
