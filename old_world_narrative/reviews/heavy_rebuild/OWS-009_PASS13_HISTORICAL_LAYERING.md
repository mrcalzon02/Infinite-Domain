# OWS-009 Pass 13 — Historical Layering

**Target:** OWS-009 — Atlas Roadside Automated Repair Depot  
**Status:** implemented for Gate-C r1

The site's history is ordinary rather than apocalyptic spectacle. Late in service, Atlas technicians faced a growing calibration-recheck backlog caused by aging drain/service joints near Bay 03 and the east support bar. They installed temporary diagnostic bypasses, tagged repeat-test stations, replaced selected lift/service collars and accumulated parts and removed cores while keeping the depot open.

D1 expresses competent maintenance under schedule pressure: the original three-cell workflow, public service, controlled records and all utilities remain operational. D3 grows only from the same deferred drain/flashing defects after abandonment; it does not invent a separate catastrophe.
