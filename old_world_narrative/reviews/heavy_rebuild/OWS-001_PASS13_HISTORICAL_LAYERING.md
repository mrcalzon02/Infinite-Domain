# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-001 — Pass 13 Historical Layering

## Canon boundary

OWS-001 is a **common Verdant Continuum Foods neighborhood service site** whose canonical collapse phase is **Pre-crisis -> Early anomaly**. Its narrative purpose is to establish that Evercrop culture distribution was ordinary, useful and ubiquitous. It must therefore begin as a convincing successful business and may not be redesigned as a sinister containment laboratory.

## D0 — intact normal operation

Gate-B r2 is the approved D0 reference. Customers enter a clean neighborhood pavilion, return cultures at the west handoff, receive refrigerated cultures at the east handoff, and never enter staff processing space. Staff move inbound product through receiving and batch/temperature check, route accepted cultures into clean cold holding/lockers, route returns west into sanitation/normal quality hold, consolidate reusable crates, and maintain rooftop refrigeration through the east service zone.

This state is deliberately mundane. `QUALITY HOLD` is normal quality-control capacity, not emergency quarantine. The company identity is optimistic and practical.

## D1 — early anomaly

D1 must remain locally understandable to workers who do **not** yet know the scale of the coming material crisis.

The first anomaly concentrates at the return and refrigeration interfaces:

- the west return stream contains a growing number of crates with degraded seals, softened liners or unexplained moisture;
- one small portion of the normal quality-hold room becomes over capacity;
- a return lane is rerouted around suspect reusable containers rather than the entire building being quarantined;
- one cold-bank/service interface receives extra inspection markings because gasket/seal replacement intervals are shortening;
- maintenance/replacement stock appears beside the sanitation and cold-service routes;
- batch/temperature check records begin recording exceptions, but normal culture issue continues;
- staff signage may gain limited temporary inspection/hold overlays, but the permanent VCF signage remains dominant.

D1 is a **maintenance and quality-control problem becoming strange**, not yet a civic emergency.

## D2 — immediate-collapse state

No dedicated D2 review state is required for OWS-001. This small common site does not need to impersonate a major containment battle. If a later authoritative D3 implementation needs one or two traces of the final evacuation/closure period, they must remain subordinate to the site's actual early-anomaly story.

## D3 — centuries-later authoritative ruin target

D3 is the present-day player-facing ruin after civilization has been gone for centuries. It is not simply D1 with more yellow blocks.

Required causal decay:

- weather penetrates roof/service joints after refrigeration maintenance ceases;
- at least one roof/service-zone failure causes localized interior water damage below rather than random holes across every roof plane;
- loading/receiving weather exposure produces debris and floor damage at the rear service edge;
- some glazing is lost or cracked while enough facade rhythm remains to identify the public entrance;
- one portion of the west return/service annex may suffer localized structural loss where water, drains and service penetrations give decay a reason to concentrate;
- failed refrigeration hardware and interrupted service pipework remain readable as remnants of the cold-chain system;
- room boundaries, the three-block staff spine, public handoff geometry and supervisor/records location remain reconstructable by the player;
- VCF identity survives in fragments strongly enough that the company/facility can still be named;
- the deterministic evidence location remains reachable.

## Explicit prohibitions

- No blanket mushroom/mycelium carpet merely because the wider collapse involved biology.
- No giant crater or military destruction at this common neighborhood site without separate canon support.
- No generic rubble pass that erases the approved D0 workflow.
- No emergency-authority redesign that belongs to later Old World institutions/sites.
- No implication that VCF knew at D0 that its ordinary culture-service model would end civilization.
