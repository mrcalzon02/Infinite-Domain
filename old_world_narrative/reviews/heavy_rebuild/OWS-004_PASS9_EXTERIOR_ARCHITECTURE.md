# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-004 Pass 9 — Exterior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-004 — Verdant Continuum Foods Mycological Vertical Farm Tower  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Exterior language

The exterior must preserve Gate-A hierarchy while turning the macro masses into a credible advanced agricultural institution. Architecture should communicate production, environmental control and public confidence before signage is read.

## Public face

The front podium remains the cleanest and most transparent elevation. Required elements include a supported arrival canopy, framed public glazing, clear entrance depth and a demonstration/showcase character distinct from service loading.

VCF lime/white identity is used as an integrated retrofit/material system, not a continuous stripe painted around the building.

## Cultivation elevations

The four production modules use:

- repeated structural bay rhythm;
- controlled horizontal glazing/clerestory bands aligned to cultivation floors;
- opaque insulated wall fields where environmental control logically requires them;
- service bands between modules;
- limited facade recesses/projections to reveal floor transitions and avoid office-box monotony.

The facade should look like a controlled-environment production tower, not a residential high-rise with balconies.

## Service elevation

The east service spine remains deliberately heavier and more mechanical. Its facade should reveal vertical utility/freight function through framed service bands, maintenance glazing, louvers/plant openings or equivalent block language. Actual operational connections are Pass 11 work, but their architectural locations must be credible.

## Receiving and dispatch

Receiving and rear dispatch require different complete dock/threshold architecture:

- framed openings;
- canopies/weather protection where appropriate;
- hardstand/apron relationship;
- service-side materials;
- clear separation from public arrival.

## Crown

The greenhouse crown must gain a repeated structural frame, controlled glazing and visible environmental-plant relationship. The adjacent plant mass should have service deck/walkway logic rather than appearing as an unrelated metal cube.

## Institutional restraint

Green/lime, white, cyan/light-blue logistics cues and yellow safety fields may appear, but no elevation should require large text labels to explain its purpose. The strongest identity should come from repeated cultivation modules, service riser, greenhouse crown and separated public/logistics base.

**EXTERIOR ARCHITECTURE: COMPLETE.**
