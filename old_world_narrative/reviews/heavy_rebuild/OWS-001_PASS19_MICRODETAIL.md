# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-001 — Pass 19 Microdetail

## Purpose

Pass 19 is limited to the final visual/operational 10–15% permitted by the Heavy Rebuild Doctrine. Gate A, Gate B r3 and Gate C r1 already define the approved massing, circulation, room program, corporate identity, historical transformation, protected routes and D3 damage logic.

Microdetail may strengthen those facts. It may not redesign them.

## Approved additions

### Cold-chain ceiling/service continuity

Add a compact overhead refrigerant/service branch in the east clean-cold zone that visibly connects the lower cold rooms to the existing vertical riser and rooftop plant. This addresses the final room-scale question of how the ceiling services reach the equipment below without creating a random pipe forest.

The branch must remain above player circulation and outside the protected three-block locker aisle.

### Sanitation utility continuity

Extend one vertical utility connection from the existing return-sanitation wet line toward the ceiling/service level. The point is to make the wash line read as installed infrastructure rather than a pipe placed on a floor.

Do not add additional unrelated technical clutter.

### Records-room work detail

The supervisor/batch-record room receives a small amount of plausible administrative equipment: a lectern/work surface and one additional archive/storage container. The guaranteed evidence chest remains the dominant progression object and stays at its approved coordinate.

### Receiving/service detail

Add only a small amount of surviving receiving-side maintenance/storage material outside the protected central staff spine and rear exit. This should make the freight edge feel worked-in without recreating the r1/r2 circulation problems.

### Existing decay remains authoritative

Do not increase the D3 damage percentage merely because Pass 19 is available. The Gate-C r1 damage distribution already passed:

- 137 changed structure positions from D0;
- 4.88% label-free floor-map change;
- roughly 1.4–1.9% visible change from each exterior camera;
- effectively complete exterior silhouette retention.

Microdetail is not permission to add more random cracks, vines, cobwebs, barrels, trapdoors or color noise.
