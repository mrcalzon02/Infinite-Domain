# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-005 Pass 10 — Interior Architecture

**Target:** OWS-005 — Verdant Continuum Foods Harvest & Packaging Annex  
**Status:** COMPLETE FOR GATE-B r1

## Raw and wet side

The raw hall contains two receiving/staging lanes and a separate lot-control zone. It connects to wet processing only through paired controlled doors in the substantial cyan sanitation spine.

Wet processing has two parallel sanitary lanes, a continuous drainage/service aisle, inspection/release positions and an enclosed normal QA/reject-hold room. Its washable floor, drainage and hygiene threshold are architectural systems rather than color decoration.

## Clean packing and cold chain

The packing high bay contains parallel line clearances, a bounded clean dry-case store and direct finished-product transfers into the cold side. It does not share the raw staging floor.

The insulated cold block is divided into two independently gated compartments with a protected center transfer aisle. Both compartments reach refrigerated dispatch without crossing packing stock or public rooms.

## Public, staff and QA rooms

The clean pavilion contains:

- visitor reception/orientation;
- a glazed process overlook;
- separate staff hygiene/change space;
- controlled staff entry;
- QA work and batch-records rooms aligned to the facade glazing.

## Readability

Floor materials, walls, doors, drainage, glazing and ceiling/frame changes communicate raw, wet, clean, cold and public/support zones before signs or machinery are considered.
