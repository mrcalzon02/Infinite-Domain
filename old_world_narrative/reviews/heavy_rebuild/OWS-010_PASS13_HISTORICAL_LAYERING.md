# OWS-010 Pass 13 — Historical Layering

**Target:** OWS-010 — Atlas Conveyor Transfer Hall  
**Status:** implemented for Gate-C r1

D0 is the exact independently accepted intact hall. D1 records a maintenance-shortage spiral rather than an unrelated emergency conversion: Lane 04 is formally locked out, selected replaceable rollers and drive modules are removed, usable parts are staged at east issue and redistributed to Lanes 01–03, and a temporary overhead drive/service bypass keeps the remaining lines operating. The Lane-04 bed, input, output, number, structural bay and service access remain legible.

D3 grows from that same decision after abandonment. The component-starved Lane-04 monitor and east service edge admit water; localized corrosion, fallen panels and debris concentrate on the already isolated line and adjacent trench. The three maintained lanes, dock hierarchy, records route and principal structure remain recognizable.

D2 is omitted because this site's end was gradual shutdown and abandonment, not a materially distinct acute collapse. A fourth state would interpolate the same unresolved roof/service leakage without adding causal evidence.
