## Rebuild intent

Phase 0 rejected the historical donor as an office-like black podium and repetitive dark tower with pasted balcony/window language, disconnected rooftop boxes and repeated interior rack motifs that did not form a credible agricultural institution. The heavy rebuild retained only the useful high-rise premise, vertical repetition opportunity and service-core potential.

The rebuilt tower is now organized as a controlled-environment production institution with a public/demo base, separate receiving and dirty-return logic, repeated cultivation modules, staff and freight circulation, environmental services, harvest/packing/dispatch, an isolatable upper production layer and a greenhouse/environmental crown.

`clean receiving -> culture/substrate staging -> freight transfer -> controlled cultivation -> harvest handoff -> inspection/grading -> packing -> outbound dispatch`

with separate public/demo, staff, dirty-return, maintenance and secondary-egress systems.

The completed tower contains:

- a clean public/demo podium distinct from industrial logistics;
- separate clean receiving and spent-material return zones;
- a rear harvest-check, packing and outbound dispatch sequence;
- four standardized cultivation decks;
- protected central production aisles;
- a genuine principal staff stair connecting the podium, all production levels and crown;
- a separate west secondary-egress ladder;
- an east freight/material-transfer system distinct from staff circulation;
- continuous environmental/nutrient service anatomy with floor branches;
- upper-floor environmental shutoff and containment-ready boundaries;
- a working greenhouse/environmental crown rather than an empty glazed cap;
- VCF identity carried by architecture and process rather than lime paint alone.
