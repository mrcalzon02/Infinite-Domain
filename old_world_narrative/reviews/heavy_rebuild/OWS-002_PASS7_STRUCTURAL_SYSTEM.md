# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-002 Pass 7 — Structural System

**Target:** OWS-002 — Verdant Continuum Foods Emergency Community Grow Hall  
**Gate-A source:** approved r2 massing  
**Status:** COMPLETE AS D0 STRUCTURAL CONTRACT

## Structural premise

The emergency conversion must preserve the logic of a civic building rather than inventing a new industrial frame behind a retained facade. The approved r2 masses therefore use two structural languages:

- retained masonry/concrete civic support wings; and
- a taller clear-span hall strengthened with visible emergency-service steel framing.

The VCF conversion adds equipment support and service framing but does not magically change every wall into a factory skeleton.

## Accepted mass hierarchy

- west/rear civic support wing: low mass, roof near Y=11;
- north public/administrative bar: intermediate mass, roof near Y=12;
- projecting public lobby: roof near Y=8;
- main grow hall: tall mass, roof near Y=18;
- glazed roof lantern: topmost feature through Y=20;
- east receiving and south dispatch: light retrofit frames;
- west/rear grow-service plant: compact equipment cluster on the lower civic roof.

No Gate-B implementation may flatten these differences.

## Civic-wing structure

The west/rear and north civic wings retain masonry perimeter bearing walls and regular internal masonry partitions. New openings should use lintel/header blocks rather than simply deleting long wall sections.

Where the emergency conversion creates a wide transfer portal between the grow hall and west harvest-wash zone, the opening must receive a visible steel or reinforced header.

## Grow-hall structure

The hall remains a civic clear-span space suitable for conversion because large community multipurpose rooms already avoid dense interior columns.

Use four principal transverse portal frames at approximately:

- Z=18;
- Z=25;
- Z=32;
- Z=39.

Each frame should read as:

- strong side-wall/post support near X=23 and X=45; and
- a high beam/truss line around Y=14–15 spanning the hall.

These frames keep the lower cultivation floor open. Do **not** drop arbitrary columns into the required three-block crop-service aisles.

## Lantern support

The roof lantern remains an original civic/daylight feature. Its corners/edges should show a believable supported transition into the hall roof using restrained steel or reinforced framing around the lantern perimeter, especially near:

- X≈27 and X≈40;
- Z≈21 and Z≈34.

The conversion may hang lighting/irrigation services from nearby hall framing, but it may not fill the glazed lantern with machinery.

## Crop-rack support

Cultivation racks are independent equipment, not primary building structure. Rack uprights may use scaffolding/steel framing and should be visibly modular. They must terminate below the hall's primary roof structure and preserve inspection/service access.

Two-tier rack structure must make upper beds plausible without creating solid opaque walls.

## Receiving frame

The east receiving retrofit requires:

- two exterior side posts;
- a clear header/canopy tied to the hall wall;
- a reinforced/recessed service opening;
- floor/apron continuity to the interior receiving zone.

It remains civic-scale and should not resemble a high-bay truck dock.

## Dispatch frame

The south relief-dispatch retrofit requires:

- opening-tied side supports;
- a narrow weather canopy;
- a clear structural header;
- a hardstanding/apron aligned to the actual dispatch route.

The canopy may not become a full-width floating slab.

## Roof grow-service support

The west/rear roof plant sits above the lower support wing. Heavy equipment masses should align over exterior/partition support lines or receive visible local frames. Separated units and maintenance gaps are mandatory.

The existing east/rear ladder and trapdoor remain the roof access route. A maintenance path across the roof may be marked later, but the ladder may not terminate beneath a solid block.

## Ceiling/service zones

- public/admin ceilings: lower, civic/institutional, around Y=7–9 where appropriate;
- west service wing: exposed localized pipe/utility runs but still civic scale;
- grow hall: high exposed frame with services around Y=11–14;
- dispatch/receiving canopies: structure visible from below.

## Gate-B structural assertions

The intact review should fail if:

- a promised hall service aisle contains a structural post;
- the lantern support is visually unsupported or covered by equipment;
- wide transfer openings have no header logic;
- receiving/dispatch canopies float without posts/headers;
- roof equipment has no service gaps;
- the roof ladder does not reach an opening/landing;
- the accepted Gate-A mass hierarchy is materially flattened.
