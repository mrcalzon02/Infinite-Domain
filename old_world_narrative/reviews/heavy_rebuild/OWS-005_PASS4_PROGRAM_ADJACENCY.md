# [SYSTEM REPORT] OWS-005 Pass 4 — Program and Adjacency

**Target:** OWS-005 — Verdant Continuum Foods Harvest & Packaging Annex  
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Site and mass hierarchy

The retained 59 x 24 x 51 envelope will support a west-to-east process campus with four interlocking volumes:

- a low covered raw-receiving shed and harvest apron on the west;
- a sanitary wet-processing and inspection hall at the center;
- a taller clean packing/case-handling hall toward the east;
- a window-poor insulated cold-hold block tied directly to finished dispatch.

A small clean QA/public pavilion faces the player approach without sharing truck circulation. Roof monitors, sanitation headers and refrigeration plant connect to the volumes they serve.

## Raw receiving / dirty zone

Required adjacencies:

- covered unloading apron;
- lot check/weigh and intake records;
- raw harvest staging;
- washable receiving threshold;
- direct feed to wash/PT-9 treatment;
- separate culled/reject/waste exit;
- no direct opening into clean packaging stock or finished cold hold.

The donor orchard side may survive only as a reduced historical/agricultural edge; it cannot remain the dominant half-site canopy.

## Wet sanitation and inspection

The central transition needs:

- staff hygiene/change entry;
- wash and PT-9 treatment lanes;
- water/chemical service and drainage trench logic;
- inspection/sample benches;
- reject-diversion spur to a controlled quality-hold room;
- clean boundary into grading/packing;
- sanitation service access that does not cross the public route.

This boundary is the architectural origin of the later anomaly response.

## Clean packing / case handling

The clean hall requires:

- grading and size/quality selection;
- parallel or staged packing lines with service clearance;
- clean cardboard/case stock arriving from a dedicated dry-store route;
- seal/coding/check point;
- pallet build and finished staging;
- protected transfer into cold hold;
- QA overlook/records adjacency.

The higher clear span should communicate line equipment and overhead services without becoming an empty warehouse volume.

## Cold chain and dispatch

The cold side requires:

- at least two legible insulated hold compartments or a subdividable cold block;
- roof/yard refrigeration equipment connected to that block;
- condensate/drain service expression;
- short protected transfer from packing to cold hold;
- finished dispatch docks on the opposite side from raw receiving;
- no public or dirty-product shortcut through the cold threshold.

## People, maintenance and exploration

Public/player approach:

`clean forecourt -> QA/visitor threshold -> controlled process overlook -> staff transition -> processing route -> QA records/proof node`

Operational routes branch from that spine:

- staff hygiene route to wet and clean zones;
- maintenance stair/walkway to roof monitors and refrigeration plant;
- raw-product route from receiving;
- clean-packaging route to dry stock;
- finished-product route to cold dispatch;
- reject route to quality hold and waste exit.

## Gate boundaries

Gate A must prove the four-volume process sequence, clean public face, opposed receiving/dispatch thresholds, cold-chain mass and roof/service hierarchy. Gate B must prove room separation, usable circulation, line clearances, service connectivity and institutional identity. Gate C may add rejected PT-9 lots and early containment history only after Gate B passes.
