# OWS-001 Visual Gate A — Massing Review Checklist

## Gate purpose

Gate A judges only whether the **D0 intact architectural massing and site occupation** are strong enough to justify expensive structural and interior development.

A successful render is not a pass. Every item below must be reviewed against the fixed-camera `ows001_fixed_v1` set and the Phase-0 baseline.

## Blocking questions

### Functional read from exterior form

- Does the structure plausibly read as neighborhood service infrastructure rather than a supermarket, warehouse, laboratory bunker, or generic commercial box?
- Can the reviewer distinguish the public entrance volume from the rear freight/service side without relying on text labels?
- Do the lower return-service annex and taller clean cold-chain block make the clean/dirty program believable at the massing level?

### Scale

- Does 39 x 13 x 33 remain credible for the stated neighborhood function?
- Is the building neither toy-sized nor institutionally oversized?
- Are roof/mechanical masses proportional to the cold-chain function they support?

### Silhouette

- Is the structure recognizably different from the Phase-0 grocery-donor silhouette?
- Do the entrance pavilion, side annexes, rear service volume and roof plant create an intentional profile from all four fixed exterior cameras?
- Is there enough asymmetry to reflect different functions without becoming arbitrary noise?

### Entrance hierarchy

- Is the public entrance immediately legible from both front three-quarter views?
- Does the entrance pavilion project/set back enough to create a real threshold?
- Is the canopy proportional rather than a decorative slab pasted onto a flat facade?

### Roof composition

- Does the roof look designed rather than forgotten?
- Does the mechanical plant sit where cold-chain service below could plausibly use it?
- Is roof equipment broken into serviceable masses rather than one giant rectangular lump?
- Does the plant improve the silhouette without making the common site look monumental?

### Service architecture

- Does the rear receiving/dispatch volume read as freight/service architecture?
- Is there a believable exterior apron/staging relationship?
- Are side service openings consistent with the zones they are intended to support?

### Site occupation

- Does the structure sit convincingly on its compact lot rather than float as an isolated box?
- Are public and rear service approach surfaces visually different enough to communicate use?
- Is there sufficient breathing room around entrances and service points?

### Future hero-space capacity

- Does the central/front volume have enough interior depth for the culture-locker/issue interface?
- Does the return annex have enough volume for sanitation, inspection and later D1 quality hold?
- Does the rear service volume have enough depth for receiving/batch check and dispatch without becoming dead space?

### Anti-detail discipline

- Has the massing pass avoided machinery clutter, loot, rubble, signage density and microdetail that could disguise a weak shell?
- Are any green VCF accents subordinate to architecture rather than doing all identity work?

## Automatic Gate-A rejection conditions

Reject and revise the massing if any of the following remain visible:

- the result still looks like the grocery donor with attachments;
- a single rectangular main box dominates every view;
- the public entrance cannot be found instantly;
- rear freight access resembles a second customer entrance;
- the roof plant looks like an arbitrary box or is disconnected from the cold-chain mass;
- return and clean sides are visually indistinguishable;
- facade depth exists only at the front while side/rear elevations remain blank slabs;
- the common neighborhood scale has accidentally become a monumental corporate facility;
- one camera angle looks convincing while the opposing angle exposes unfinished or meaningless massing.

## Gate record

When the r1 massing set exists, record:

- baseline comparison findings;
- front-left findings;
- front-right findings;
- rear-left findings;
- rear-right findings;
- roof/top findings;
- interior/cutaway volume findings;
- dimensions accepted or reopened;
- visible defects requiring r2;
- final Gate-A decision: `PASSED` or `REVISION REQUIRED`.

Do not begin structural system, detailed circulation, interior architecture or operational machinery until Gate A is passed.
