## Fixed historical facts

Treat these as immutable unless the project owner explicitly revises the canon bible:

- EP-7 / **Mycella permanens EP-7**, commercial name **Evercrop**, was engineered as an industrial food fungus.
- It was intentionally durable, nutritious, fast-growing, transportable, easily seeded/reseeded, long-lived, environmentally tolerant, and resistant to ordinary crop collapse.
- PT-9 / **Plastovorax tutela PT-9** was intentionally paired with EP-7 as a protective bacterial symbiont.
- PT-9 protected the fungus against competing microorganisms.
- PT-9 could also degrade synthetic polymers. That behavior became catastrophic through symbiosis.
- EP-7 gave PT-9 mobility, distribution, moisture, shelter, and access to a worldwide logistics network.
- PT-9 compromised seals, membranes, insulation, coatings, filters, and other synthetic barriers, opening paths for EP-7.
- EVERCROP was a real commercial success before it became a disaster and was distributed globally.
- APL-3 / **Aevum Peptide Lattice** provided genuine regenerative and life-extension benefits.
- Medical demand for APL-derived treatment increased dependence on and cultivation of the Evercrop ecosystem.
- The Continuity Working Group formed from multiple small scientific/technical teams, not one omniscient whistleblower.
- Dr. Mara Vey understood EP-7 distribution and durability.
- Dr. Elias Thorne understood PT-9 polymer degradation.
- Dr. Sora Kel understood systemic material failure.
- Dr. Aron Vale understood atmospheric transport.
- Dr. Lena Orlov understood APL-3 and civilization's dependence on the biological system.
- Continuity recognized a **Distributed Reservoir** problem: civilization no longer had isolated outbreaks but countless hidden reservoirs throughout logistics, infrastructure, products, farms, filters, drains, vehicles, buildings, and storage.
- The organism had not merely escaped one laboratory. By the time society recognized the catastrophe, meaningful global containment had already ceased to exist.
- Quarantine could still slow local spread and buy time; it could not recreate a clean planet.
- Fire could kill colonies locally; mass burning also created transport opportunities for contaminated material.
- Nuclear Firebreak strikes could sterilize local target zones. Their strategic failure was atmospheric redistribution, not an inability to kill the organism at ground zero.
- Viable EP-7/PT-9 was eventually detected above nineteen kilometers. This is a major narrative proof point.
- The Firebreak Wars grew out of the containment crisis, failed communications, suspicion, border sterilization, retaliation, and state fragmentation. They are not an unrelated apocalypse pasted onto the end.
- The central wasteland is heavily damaged partly because industrial density provided abundant substrate; remote northern/southern ecological refugia recovered better.
- Modern Spore organisms descend from the EP-7/PT-9 catastrophe but have had centuries to evolve beyond what Old World scientists knew.
- APL-3 genuinely worked. The eventual question is whether its benefits can be recovered without recreating the biological conditions of the collapse.
- Charles guides reconstruction but does not begin with a complete history. He reconstructs meaning alongside the player.
- The player's progression intentionally retraces the technological ladder of the civilization whose ruins surround them.

## Narrative framing

The Old World was not destroyed because everyone was stupid or technology was useless. The tragedy is that competent institutions repeatedly found short-term measures that seemed rational, necessary, or even successful while the underlying system had already changed beyond the assumptions those measures depended on.

The player should be able to understand why:

- VCF wanted a crop that could not easily fail;
- Aevum wanted to continue life-saving and life-extending treatment;
- governments feared famine and panic;
- military commands tried to save cities and strategic regions;
- logistics companies kept food and medicine moving;
- materials engineers kept promising improved containment;
- Continuity had incomplete evidence and could not simply order the world to stop.

## World-storytelling rule

Different locations own different kinds of history:

- VCF farms/labs — agriculture, EP-7, PT-9, commercial success, early anomalies.
- Aevum — regenerative medicine, longevity, dependence, moral cost of abandoning the system.
- PolyCore — seals, polymers, material substitution, infrastructure failure.
- Atlas — automation and the mechanical sophistication of the Old World.
- Pleroma/ports — global distribution, shipping, cold chain, contamination movement.
- Helion — power infrastructure and cascading service failure.
- military/Firebreak — impossible operational decisions and local sterilization success.
- Continuity — cross-disciplinary synthesis and the Distributed Reservoir model.
- Blackglass — encrypted political/corporate history and Darknet re-exploration.
- civilian sites — fear, routine, rumor, family, dependence, evacuation, ordinary life.
- Asterion/orbit — records Earth could no longer preserve and the continuation of the archaeological story into space.

Never put the complete explanation in one building.

## Artistic rule: the Old World must look worth saving

Some surviving spaces should look efficient, pleasant, clean, prosperous, or genuinely humane. The player needs to see advanced hospitals, comfortable apartments, functional transit, clean markets, sophisticated industry, parks, schools, libraries, restaurants, and attractive clinics. A civilization that was always an obvious hellscape has less emotional weight when it is lost.

## Charles arc

Early Charles: focused on survival, production, automation, and recovery.

Middle Charles: recognizes repeating industrial patterns, EP-7 references, material failures, and the existence of Continuity.

Late terrestrial Charles: understands that the central failure was not a perimeter breach but the disappearance of a meaningful perimeter.

Darknet Charles: can correlate previously isolated records and reconstruct political/strategic history.

Space Charles: uses orbital evidence to finish portions of the terrestrial record and confronts the fact that the player is rebuilding along the same technological ascent.

Do not let Charles spoil discoveries before the player has evidence.
